Marine & Port Supplies in Singapore

PANDA COLLECTIONS sources mooring and berthing equipment, fenders, cargo lashing, lifting and rigging, marine hoses, pumps and valves, port electrical and lighting, spill response and terminal maintenance supplies for ports, terminals, shipyards and marine contractors.

PANDA COLLECTIONS, the procurement brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD., supports marine and port procurement in Singapore and the region. The range covers mooring and berthing equipment, port and terminal handling equipment, cargo securing and lashing, marine hoses and fluid transfer, marine pumps, valves and piping, port electrical and lighting, fenders, buoys and navigation aids, dock, quay and facility maintenance, lifting, rigging and inspection, spill control and environmental protection, and warehouse and yard operations.

We work with port operators, container and bulk terminals, shipyards, marine engineering contractors and logistics facilities. Buyers can send the equipment model, load requirements, dimensions, material, class or project specification and the delivery date; PANDA COLLECTIONS coordinates supplier quotations, document review, quality checks and delivery.

For lifting, mooring and load-bearing items, safe working load (SWL / WLL), breaking load, certificates and inspection requirements are confirmed for each specific product before supply.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What marine and port supplies does PANDA COLLECTIONS source?

Mooring ropes and berthing equipment, fenders and buoys, container lashing and cargo securing equipment, lifting and rigging gear, marine hoses, pumps, valves and piping, port electrical and lighting, spill response products and dock and terminal maintenance consumables.

Can you supply lifting and mooring equipment with certificates?

Certificates, rated loads and inspection documents are confirmed for the specific product offered. State the SWL / WLL, breaking load and certificate requirements in the enquiry.

Who do you supply in the marine sector?

Port operators, container and bulk terminals, shipyards, marine engineering contractors, ship managers and logistics facilities in Singapore and international markets.

Is Marine & Port different from Ship Chandlery?

Yes. Marine & Port Supplies covers port, terminal and shipyard equipment and maintenance; Ship Chandlery & Vessel Maintenance covers stores, consumables and spares used on board vessels.
