FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What do Type 4, 5 and 6 mean on a coverall?

They are European chemical-protective clothing types: Type 4 spray-tight, Type 5 protection against airborne dry particles and Type 6 limited splash protection.

Can you build a PPE kit to our list?

Yes. Protective kits can combine a coverall, hood, mask, face shield and shoe and boot covers in a kit bag or box to your specification.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Request a quote with the product codes, quantities and delivery location, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
