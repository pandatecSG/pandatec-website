PANDATEC supplies medical furniture, hospital equipment and laboratory furniture to hospitals, clinics and laboratories in Singapore. The range covers hospital beds, medical trolleys carts, stretchers, examination treatment tables, wheelchairs stools, waiting chairs, medical cabinets, stainless steel furniture, laboratory furniture, medical consumables. Each enquiry starts with the intended application and the requirements that matter to your organisation. You can browse a subcategory, select relevant products for a quote, or describe a requirement that is not yet listed.

For a useful sourcing brief, include quantities, dimensions, materials, preferred finishes and the delivery location where these are relevant. Drawings, reference photographs and a bill of quantities can help explain a project. Identify any mandatory standards or documentation needed for your intended use so these can be reviewed for the specific product.

PANDATEC coordinates sourcing, procurement, supplier communication, quality control and logistics. Customisation, delivery timing, installation support and any required product evidence are confirmed individually. The catalogue should be used as a starting point for an enquiry; it does not establish stock availability or certify suitability. If several product categories are involved, include the overall project scope so consolidation and delivery requirements can be considered together.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Are your medical products HSA-registered?

No blanket registration claim is made. Identify the exact product and intended use, and request its relevant documentation for review by your responsible team. Sample listings are not evidence of registration or approval.

Can hospital beds be customised?

Provide the required configuration, dimensions and intended application. A supplier can assess the request, but feasibility and the effect of changes on product documentation must be confirmed before an order.

Do you supply laboratory fume hoods and benches?

Laboratory furniture enquiries are included in the range. Send the technical brief and site constraints established by your responsible specialists. Performance, installation and any required testing must be expressly defined for the proposed equipment.
