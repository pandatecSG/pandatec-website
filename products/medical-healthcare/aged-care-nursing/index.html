MEDICAL & HEALTHCARE

Aged Care & Nursing

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies aged-care and nursing products for nursing homes, senior living communities, hospitals, rehabilitation centres, day-care centres and home care, in Singapore and worldwide: electric lift recliners and transfer lifts, home-style and smart vital-sign monitoring nursing beds, solid-ash aged-care furniture, bedroom and bathroom safety aids, NB-IoT safety sensors, walking aids and wheelchairs, rehabilitation training equipment and recreation tables.

Key features

  • Electric, rotating and vital-sign monitoring nursing beds
  • Lift recliners and sit-to-stand transfer lifts
  • Solid-ash aged-care furniture with 450 mm seat heights and grip points
  • Toilet lift chairs, commodes, shower chairs and grab rails
  • Wheelchairs, rollators, rehab equipment and activity tables

Specifications: typical values, confirmed per order; custom sizes, wood finishes and upholstery colours on request. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What products does a nursing home need?

Typical fit-outs combine nursing beds with pressure-care mattresses, bedside cabinets, transfer aids, bathroom safety products, dining and lounge furniture with raised seat heights, mobility aids and activity equipment. Send the room count and care level for a recommended list.

Can aged-care furniture be made in our colours?

Yes. Wood finishes, upholstery and faux-leather colours can be customised per project.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Request a quote with the product codes, quantities and delivery location, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
