PANDA COLLECTIONS, the procurement brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD., supports ship owners, ship management companies, ship agents, shipyards and marine service providers with vessel supply and maintenance procurement. The range covers deck stores and mooring, engine-room consumables, pumps, valves and engine spares, safety and life-saving equipment, navigation and communication accessories, cabin, galley and accommodation stores, marine cleaning chemicals, paints and coatings, rope, rigging and general hardware, waste handling and environmental stores, and welding and repair materials.

Send the requisition by vessel name and type, maker and model, part number, quantity, port or anchorage, ETA / ETD and delivery deadline, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will coordinate multi-category quotations and delivery.

Genuine OEM spares are not assumed: each spare part is confirmed by maker, model and part number with traceability. Safety, firefighting and life-saving equipment is checked against SOLAS, flag state or class requirements where these apply, with valid certificates for the item supplied.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What ship chandlery supplies can PANDA COLLECTIONS source?

Deck stores, engine-room consumables, pumps, valves, filters and spares, ropes and rigging, safety and life-saving equipment, navigation accessories, marine cleaning chemicals, paints and coatings, welding and repair materials, waste handling stores and cabin, galley and accommodation stores.

What should a ship supply requisition include?

Vessel name, IMO number if applicable, port or anchorage, ETA / ETD, delivery deadline, maker and model, part number, quantity, photos and any certificate requirements.

Do you supply original manufacturer spare parts?

Spare parts are quoted line by line against the maker, model and part number. Whether an item is original or compatible, and its traceability, is stated in the quotation.

Can you deliver to vessels in Singapore?

Delivery to port or anchorage is arranged per order and confirmed against the vessel's ETA / ETD when the quotation is accepted.
