Electrical & Hazardous Area Equipment

PANDA COLLECTIONS, the procurement brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD. in Singapore, sources electrical & hazardous area equipment for business buyers, including ex motors, lighting, junction boxes, cable glands. Each enquiry is quoted to your specification, with supplier selection, quality checks before shipment and delivery coordination in Singapore and international markets.

Typical products

  • Explosion-proof motors and lighting
  • Ex junction boxes and cable glands
  • Cable trays and industrial cables
  • Switchgear, MCC and UPS
  • Earthing and lightning protection

What to include in your RFQ

  • BOQ or item list with quantities
  • Material grade, size, pressure class and end connections (e.g. ASME, EN, JIS)
  • Process medium, temperature and pressure
  • Equipment maker, model and part number for spares
  • Required documents: MTC, test reports, datasheets, certificates
  • Delivery location, required date and Incoterms

Documents: Material grades, pressure ratings, hazardous-area certification (such as ATEX or IECEx), SIL ratings and test documents are confirmed for each specific model and project; no certification is assumed without documents for the offered item. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What electrical & hazardous area equipment can PANDA COLLECTIONS source?

Typical items include explosion-proof motors and lighting; ex junction boxes and cable glands; cable trays and industrial cables; switchgear, mcc and ups; earthing and lightning protection. Items outside this list can be sourced to your specification.

What should an enquiry for electrical & hazardous area equipment include?

Include bOQ or item list with quantities; material grade, size, pressure class and end connections (e.g. ASME, EN, JIS); process medium, temperature and pressure; equipment maker, model and part number for spares; required documents: MTC, test reports, datasheets, certificates; delivery location, required date and Incoterms.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on specification, quantity, documents and delivery terms. Send your requirement and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
