Oil, Gas & Petrochemical Supplies in Singapore

PANDA COLLECTIONS sources piping, valves, pumps, pressure equipment, instrumentation, electrical, coatings, welding consumables and plant maintenance supplies for refineries, petrochemical and chemical plants, terminals and EPC contractors.

PANDA COLLECTIONS, the procurement brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD., is a Singapore-based B2B sourcing and supply chain partner for oil refineries, petrochemical and chemical plants, oil and gas storage terminals, EPC contractors and plant maintenance teams. The range covers piping and pipeline materials, industrial valves and flow control, pumps, compressors and mechanical equipment, static and pressure equipment, instrumentation and process control, electrical and hazardous-area equipment, welding, fabrication and NDT, sealing, gaskets and fasteners, corrosion protection and coatings, plant maintenance and turnaround MRO, petrochemical and industrial chemicals, storage and terminal systems, and fire, gas and emergency response supplies.

Buyers can send a BOQ, equipment models, material grades, pressure ratings, process media, quantities and the delivery location. PANDA COLLECTIONS coordinates manufacturer quotations, technical clarification, quality checks before shipment and logistics for project and recurring procurement.

Material grades, pressure ratings, hazardous-area certification (such as ATEX or IECEx), SIL ratings and test documents are confirmed for each specific model and project; no certification is assumed without documents for the offered item.
