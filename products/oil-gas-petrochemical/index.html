PANDA COLLECTIONS, the procurement brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD., is a Singapore-based B2B sourcing and supply chain partner for oil refineries, petrochemical and chemical plants, oil and gas storage terminals, EPC contractors and plant maintenance teams. The range covers piping and pipeline materials, industrial valves and flow control, pumps, compressors and mechanical equipment, static and pressure equipment, instrumentation and process control, electrical and hazardous-area equipment, welding, fabrication and NDT, sealing, gaskets and fasteners, corrosion protection and coatings, plant maintenance and turnaround MRO, petrochemical and industrial chemicals, storage and terminal systems, and fire, gas and emergency response supplies.

Buyers can send a BOQ, equipment models, material grades, pressure ratings, process media, quantities and the delivery location. PANDA COLLECTIONS coordinates manufacturer quotations, technical clarification, quality checks before shipment and logistics for project and recurring procurement.

Material grades, pressure ratings, hazardous-area certification (such as ATEX or IECEx), SIL ratings and test documents are confirmed for each specific model and project; no certification is assumed without documents for the offered item.

What to Include in Your Enquiry

  • BOQ or item list with quantities
  • Material grade, size, pressure class and end connections (e.g. ASME, EN, JIS)
  • Process medium, temperature and pressure
  • Equipment maker, model and part number for spares

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What oil and gas supplies does PANDA COLLECTIONS source?

Piping and fittings, flanges, industrial valves, pumps and compressors, pressure-system components, instrumentation, hazardous-area electrical equipment, welding and NDT consumables, gaskets and fasteners, industrial coatings, turnaround MRO, petrochemicals and terminal and emergency-response supplies for refineries, chemical plants and EPC projects.

Can you quote against a BOQ or turnaround material list?

Yes. Send the BOQ or material list with material grades, sizes, pressure classes, quantities and required documents. Items are quoted per line, and alternatives are only proposed where you allow them.

Do you supply valves and pipes with mill test certificates?

Material test certificates, test reports and datasheets are requested from the manufacturer for the offered item. State the documents you need in the enquiry so they can be confirmed before the order.

Do you supply outside Singapore?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS is headquartered in Singapore and coordinates delivery to plants, terminals and project sites in Singapore and international markets.
