Nickel Alloy Welding Wire

Nickel alloy welding wire is filler metal for TIG, MIG and submerged-arc welding of nickel alloys, dissimilar metals and cast heat-resistant tubes. The range covers AWS A5.14 ERNiCrMo-3 (625), ERNiCr-3 (82), ERNiCu-7 (Alloy 400 filler) and ERNiCrMo-4 (C-276), plus 25/35 Nb, 35/45 Nb and 28/48 W5 fillers for cracking- and reformer-furnace tubes.

Key features

  • AWS A5.14 nickel alloy filler metals
  • Straight TIG rods Ø2.0 / 2.4 / 3.2 mm
  • Spooled MIG wire Ø0.9 / 1.0 / 1.2 / 1.6 mm
  • Fillers for cracking and reformer furnace tubes
  • Dissimilar-metal and overlay welding

Standards & documents: ASTM / AMS / AWS / GB product standards as listed per product; EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificates, heat traceability, ESR/VAR melting and NDT confirmed per enquiry. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which filler wire is used to weld Alloy 800H?

ERNiCr-3 (AWS A5.14) is commonly used for Alloy 800/800H/800HT and for dissimilar joints to stainless and carbon steel.

Which wire is used to weld Alloy 625?

ERNiCrMo-3 (AWS A5.14) is the matching filler for Alloy 625 and is also used for Alloy 825 and 6Mo stainless steels.
