Special Alloys

Special alloys are nickel-, cobalt- and iron-based metals engineered for high temperature, corrosion, electrical resistance or controlled expansion where standard carbon and stainless steels fail. PANDA COLLECTIONS sources special alloy plate, bar, strip, wire, forgings and welding consumables for refineries, furnace builders, power, marine, electronics and aerospace buyers in Singapore and the region, with mill test certificates and testing confirmed per order.

Key features

  • Nickel & corrosion-resistant alloys: Alloy 800H, 825, 625, 600, 601, 400, K-500, C-276, 904L, duplex 2205 / 2507
  • Superalloys: GH3030, GH4169 (Alloy 718), GH4145 (Alloy X-750), GH2132 (A-286), L605
  • Resistance heating alloys: NiCr 80/20, 70/30, 60/15, 35/20 and FeCrAl wire, strip and furnace elements
  • Precision alloys: soft magnetic, glass-sealing, low-expansion and elastic alloys
  • Heat-resistant stainless steels 310S, 314, 309S, 321 and nickel alloy welding wire to AWS A5.14

Standards & documents: ASTM / AMS / AWS / GB product standards as listed per product; EN 10204 3.1 mill test certificates, heat traceability, ESR/VAR melting and NDT confirmed per enquiry. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What are special alloys used for?

They are used where heat, corrosion or precise physical properties rule out ordinary steel: furnace and petrochemical heater parts, chemical process equipment, seawater systems, heating elements, electronic seals and aerospace components.

Can you supply to a project material specification?

Yes. Send the specification with grade, product form, sizes and testing requirements (for example ESR/VAR melting, grain size, ultrasonic testing or stress-rupture tests). Any deviation is stated in the quotation before the order is placed.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on grade, size, quantity, melting route, testing and certificates. Request a quote with the product codes and requirements, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
