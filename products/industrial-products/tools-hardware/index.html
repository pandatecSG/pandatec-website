BUYING GUIDE

How to choose tools & hardware

When comparing tools & hardware for a business, check:

  • Task and material
  • Corded or cordless platform
  • Quality grade for daily use
  • Spare parts and accessories

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies tools & hardware to workshops, factories, contractors and maintenance teams in Singapore and worldwide. Include quantities, specifications and the delivery location in your enquiry.

What should I check when buying tools & hardware?

Check task and material, corded or cordless platform, quality grade for daily use and spare parts and accessories. Confirm the details for each model on its product page or in the quotation.

Which businesses buy tools & hardware?

Typical buyers include workshops, factories, contractors and maintenance teams.
