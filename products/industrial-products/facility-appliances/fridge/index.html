BUYING GUIDE

How to choose fridges & freezers

When comparing fridges & freezers for a business, check:

  • Capacity in litres
  • Type (top freezer, bottom freezer, side-by-side, multi-door, bar fridge)
  • NEA energy label ticks
  • Dimensions for the space

What should I check when buying fridges & freezers?

Check capacity in litres, type (top freezer, bottom freezer, side-by-side, multi-door, bar fridge), NEA energy label ticks and dimensions for the space. Confirm the details for each model on its product page or in the quotation.

Which businesses buy fridges & freezers?

Typical buyers include office pantries, hotels and serviced apartments, staff accommodation and clinics.
