PANDATEC supplies industrial consumables, MRO products, tools and workshop equipment to factories, workshops and facilities in Singapore. The range covers mro consumables, tools hardware, fasteners, workshop equipment, safety storage cabinets, electrical accessories, cleaning maintenance, facility appliances. Each enquiry starts with the intended application and the requirements that matter to your organisation. You can browse a subcategory, select relevant products for a quote, or describe a requirement that is not yet listed.

For a useful sourcing brief, include quantities, dimensions, materials, preferred finishes and the delivery location where these are relevant. Drawings, reference photographs and a bill of quantities can help explain a project. Identify any mandatory standards or documentation needed for your intended use so these can be reviewed for the specific product.

PANDATEC coordinates sourcing, procurement, supplier communication, quality control and logistics. Customisation, delivery timing, installation support and any required product evidence are confirmed individually. The catalogue should be used as a starting point for an enquiry; it does not establish stock availability or certify suitability. If several product categories are involved, include the overall project scope so consolidation and delivery requirements can be considered together.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply fire safety and explosion-proof cabinets?

These requirements fall within the safety-storage enquiry range. Specify the intended contents and the standards your responsible team requires. Suitability and documentary evidence must be checked for the exact offered cabinet; no blanket rating is claimed.

Can you consolidate MRO orders from multiple suppliers?

Consolidation can be discussed for a mixed order. Provide item references, quantities, supplier readiness and handling requirements. The proposed checking, packing and shipment scope needs confirmation.

Do you support recurring monthly supply?

Share the repeat item list, expected consumption and ordering frequency. A recurring arrangement can be discussed, but stock commitments, minimum quantities, review dates and delivery schedules must be agreed separately.
