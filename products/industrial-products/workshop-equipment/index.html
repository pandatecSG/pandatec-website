BUYING GUIDE

How to choose workshop equipment

When comparing workshop equipment for a business, check:

  • Load capacity
  • Drawer and storage layout
  • Worktop material
  • Mobility and locking

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies workshop equipment to workshops, factories, laboratories and maintenance rooms in Singapore and worldwide. Include quantities, specifications and the delivery location in your enquiry.

Request a business quote

What should I check when buying workshop equipment?

Check load capacity, drawer and storage layout, worktop material and mobility and locking. Confirm the details for each model on its product page or in the quotation.

Which businesses buy workshop equipment?

Typical buyers include workshops, factories, laboratories and maintenance rooms.

How do I get a quotation for workshop equipment in Singapore?

Prices are not published. Send PANDA COLLECTIONS the product codes or specifications, quantity and delivery country and address; we can also source models that are not listed.
