BUYING GUIDE

How to choose safety shoes

When comparing safety shoes for a business, check:

  • Standard such as SS 513, EN ISO 20345 or ASTM F2413
  • Slip and puncture resistance
  • Electrical or ESD properties
  • Size and comfort

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies safety shoes to factories, construction sites, warehouses and logistics in Singapore and worldwide. Include quantities, specifications and the delivery location in your enquiry.

What should I check when buying safety shoes?

Check standard such as SS 513, EN ISO 20345 or ASTM F2413, slip and puncture resistance, electrical or ESD properties and size and comfort. Confirm the details for each model on its product page or in the quotation.

Which businesses buy safety shoes?

Typical buyers include factories, construction sites, warehouses and logistics.
