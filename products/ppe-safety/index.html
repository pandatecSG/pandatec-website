PANDATEC supplies personal protective equipment and workplace safety products to construction, industrial and healthcare organisations in Singapore. The range covers respiratory protection, gloves, protective clothing coveralls, safety shoes, eye face protection, head protection, hearing protection, fall protection. Each enquiry starts with the intended application and the requirements that matter to your organisation. You can browse a subcategory, select relevant products for a quote, or describe a requirement that is not yet listed.

For a useful sourcing brief, include quantities, dimensions, materials, preferred finishes and the delivery location where these are relevant. Drawings, reference photographs and a bill of quantities can help explain a project. Identify any mandatory standards or documentation needed for your intended use so these can be reviewed for the specific product.

PANDATEC coordinates sourcing, procurement, supplier communication, quality control and logistics. Customisation, delivery timing, installation support and any required product evidence are confirmed individually. The catalogue should be used as a starting point for an enquiry; it does not establish stock availability or certify suitability. If several product categories are involved, include the overall project scope so consolidation and delivery requirements can be considered together.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between N95, KN95 and FFP2?

These labels refer to different respirator classifications and should not be treated as automatic substitutes. Ask your responsible safety team to specify the required classification and application, then review product-specific evidence. This catalogue does not give selection or fit advice.

Do you supply PPE in bulk for projects?

Share the product list, required classifications, sizes, quantities and delivery schedule. Bulk project enquiries can be discussed, with minimum quantities, availability and required documents confirmed for the proposed items.

Which certifications do your PPE products have?

Only verified product-specific evidence should be used. Specify the standard required by your safety team and request the complete supporting document for the exact offered item. No certification is claimed for the sample records.
