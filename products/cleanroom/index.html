PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies cleanroom consumables, garments and ESD products to pharmaceutical, semiconductor and electronics facilities in Singapore. The range covers cleanroom garments, cleanroom gloves, masks shoe covers, cleanroom wipes, cleanroom bags packaging, esd products, cleanroom furniture storage. Each enquiry starts with the intended application and the requirements that matter to your organisation. You can browse a subcategory, select relevant products for a quote, or describe a requirement that is not yet listed.

For a useful sourcing brief, include quantities, dimensions, materials, preferred finishes and the delivery location where these are relevant. Drawings, reference photographs and a bill of quantities can help explain a project. Identify any mandatory standards or documentation needed for your intended use so these can be reviewed for the specific product.

PANDA COLLECTIONS coordinates sourcing, procurement, supplier communication, quality control and logistics. Customisation, delivery timing, installation support and any required product evidence are confirmed individually. The catalogue should be used as a starting point for an enquiry; it does not establish stock availability or certify suitability. If several product categories are involved, include the overall project scope so consolidation and delivery requirements can be considered together.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which ISO cleanroom classes do your products suit?

Suitability must be assessed for the particular item and your process requirements. State the classification and evidence required by your technical team. No cleanroom-class compatibility is asserted for an unverified sample.

Do you supply ESD chairs and anti-static mats?

ESD products are included as an enquiry category. Share dimensions, intended use and the performance criteria your team requires. Ask for supporting information for the exact proposed product rather than relying on its category name.

Can you provide certificates of conformance?

Identify the product, order or batch information and the document your approval process needs. Availability and scope must be confirmed with the proposed supplier; the website does not promise a certificate for every item.
