CLEANROOM & CONTROLLED ENVIRONMENT

Cleanroom Furniture & Storage

Cleanroom furniture and storage covers the seating and work-area surfaces used inside cleanrooms and electrostatic protected areas (EPAs). The range includes ESD chairs and stools that ground the seated operator through conductive upholstery, frames and castors or glides, and ESD floor mats, anti-fatigue mats and 2-layer rubber bench mats that ground operators, chairs and worktops to a common earth point. Lab steel wall benches and stainless steel work tables complete the workstation.

Key features

  • ESD chairs with backrest, adjustable height and optional footring
  • ESD round four-leg stools, plastic-back chairs and foam chairs and stools
  • Conductive castors or glides for a ground path through ESD flooring
  • ESD anti-fatigue floor mats for standing workstations
  • ESD antistatic rubber mats and 2-layer conductive bench mats for worktops
  • Supplied for electronics assembly, SMT, semiconductor and laboratory EPAs

Supply: PANDA COLLECTIONS sources and quality-checks these products for buyers in Singapore and the region; test reports, RoHS/REACH declarations and certificates are confirmed per enquiry. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a chair ESD-safe?

An ESD chair provides a continuous conductive path from the seat and backrest through the frame and conductive castors or glides to an ESD floor or floor mat. Seating resistance is tested to ANSI/ESD STM12.1 and is typically required to be below 1 × 10^9 Ω to ground.

Do ESD chairs need ESD flooring or floor mats?

Yes. An ESD chair drains charge through its castors or glides, so it must stand on an ESD floor or a grounded ESD floor mat; on ordinary flooring the chair is not grounded.

Which mat should go on an ESD worktop?

Use a 2-layer rubber bench mat with a static-dissipative top layer and a conductive bottom layer, connected to the common ground point with a ground cord. ESD anti-fatigue mats are for standing positions on the floor.
