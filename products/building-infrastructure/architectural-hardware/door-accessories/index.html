Door Accessories

Door accessories that complete a door set: dust-proof floor sockets for flush bolts, tubular latches and deadbolt latches, push/pull door plates, door coordinators for double doors, compact surface and concealed door closers, bathroom thumb-turn roses and sliding-door locks, and wide-angle door viewers. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies them to businesses in Singapore and worldwide.

Key features

  • Dust-proof floor sockets for flush bolts
  • Tubular latches with bevel or square bolt
  • Door coordinators for pairs of doors
  • Door viewers with 180°–200° view

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Why fit a dust-proof floor socket?

It gives a flush bolt a clean hole in the floor and springs closed when the bolt is withdrawn, so dirt does not block the socket.

What does a door coordinator do?

On a pair of doors with a rebate, it holds the active leaf open until the inactive leaf has closed, so both leaves close in the right order.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Send the model codes, finish, quantity and delivery country for a quotation.
