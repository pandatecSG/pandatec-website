Architectural Hardware

Our architectural hardware range covers the fittings that make a door work: concealed, pivot and flag hinges, door stops and holders, flush bolts, latches and door guards, ring pulls and thumb turns, door accessories such as viewers, latches and coordinators, and automatic door bottom seals. We supply architectural hardware to hotels, offices, hospitals, schools and residential and commercial fit-outs in Singapore and worldwide, with product selection, quality checks before shipment and delivery coordination. Share the product codes, finish, quantity and delivery location when requesting a quotation.
