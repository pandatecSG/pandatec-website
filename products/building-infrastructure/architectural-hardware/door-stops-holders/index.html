Door Stops & Holders

Door stops and door holders that protect walls and keep doors open: floor-mounted half-dome and cylinder stops, wall-mounted stops, magnetic door holders and floor magnets, adjustable-height stops, weighted floor stops, door wedges and lever-operated door holders, in stainless steel, zinc alloy and aluminium. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies them to businesses in Singapore and worldwide.

Key features

  • Floor, wall and skirting-mounted styles
  • Magnetic holders keep doors open
  • Stainless steel, zinc alloy and aluminium
  • Satin, polished, antique and PVD finishes

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Floor-mounted or wall-mounted door stop?

Wall-mounted stops suit doors that open onto a wall; floor-mounted stops suit doors that swing into open space or where skirting is in the way.

When do I need a magnetic door holder?

Use a magnetic door holder where a door should stay open, for example in corridors, meeting rooms and back-of-house areas. Fire doors need holders approved for the fire-door system.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Send the model codes, finish, quantity and delivery country for a quotation.
