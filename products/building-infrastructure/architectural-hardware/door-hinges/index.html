Door Hinges

Door hinges for timber, aluminium and steel doors, hidden flush doors and cabinets: zinc alloy and stainless steel concealed cross hinges, three-way (3D) adjustable invisible hinges, heavy-duty hinges for stone-clad doors, top-and-bottom pivots, flag hinges and centre and offset pivot hinges. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies them to businesses in Singapore and worldwide.

Key features

  • Concealed hinges with up to 180° opening
  • 3D adjustment of height, side and depth on site
  • Zinc alloy, die-cast or investment-cast 304 stainless steel
  • Satin, polished, antique and PVD finishes

Specifications: listed per model; finishes, lengths and packing confirmed per order. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which hinge suits a hidden (flush) door?

Use a concealed cross hinge or a 3D adjustable concealed hinge. Choose the size by door thickness and weight; heavier stone-clad doors need the heavy-duty stone-door hinges.

How many hinges does a door need?

Most concealed-hinge doors use two or three hinges; doors taller than 2100 mm usually need a fourth. Check the load rating of each model against the door weight.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Send the model codes, finish, quantity and delivery country for a quotation.
