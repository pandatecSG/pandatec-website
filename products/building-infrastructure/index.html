PANDATEC sources building, construction and infrastructure materials from manufacturers across Asia for contractors and developers in Singapore. The range covers pipes, cables electrical, construction hardware, architectural hardware, fencing barriers, road traffic products, paints coatings, boards panels, flooring, signage, metal products. Each enquiry starts with the intended application and the requirements that matter to your organisation. You can browse a subcategory, select relevant products for a quote, or describe a requirement that is not yet listed.

For a useful sourcing brief, include quantities, dimensions, materials, preferred finishes and the delivery location where these are relevant. Drawings, reference photographs and a bill of quantities can help explain a project. Identify any mandatory standards or documentation needed for your intended use so these can be reviewed for the specific product.

PANDATEC coordinates sourcing, procurement, supplier communication, quality control and logistics. Customisation, delivery timing, installation support and any required product evidence are confirmed individually. The catalogue should be used as a starting point for an enquiry; it does not establish stock availability or certify suitability. If several product categories are involved, include the overall project scope so consolidation and delivery requirements can be considered together.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you supply to project specifications and BOQ?

Send the current bill of quantities, item references and specification revisions. Identify mandatory requirements and whether alternatives may be proposed. Any offer must be reviewed against your project approval process.

Do your products come with test reports or certificates?

Availability depends on the particular product and supplier. Name the documents required, and request evidence that matches the offered item. An unrelated report or category-level description should not be treated as confirmation.

Can you source building materials from China for Singapore projects?

PANDATEC sources products from manufacturers across China and Asia. Share the material specification, quantities and destination requirements so that a suitable sourcing and delivery scope can be discussed.
