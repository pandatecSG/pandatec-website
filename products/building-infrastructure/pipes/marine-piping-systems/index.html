Marine Piping Systems

Thermoplastic water supply and drainage piping for ship accommodation and superstructure: black PE100 pipe and fittings for grey and black water drainage, grey PE-RT for hot and cold potable water and green PP-R for fresh water. The range covers socket and butt fusion elbows, equal, reducing and 45° Y-tees, crosses, reducers, couplings, end caps, an access port, flange adaptors and flange plates to GB2506 and GB573, female, male and union threaded transition fittings, rubber-lined pipe clamps and angle supports. The systems follow IMO A.753(18) and A.653(16) and hold type approval from major classification societies. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies shipyards, marine outfitting contractors and offshore projects in Singapore and worldwide.

Key features

  • PE100 drainage, PE-RT and PP-R hot and cold water
  • Type approved by ABS, DNV, LR, BV, CCS, NK and KR
  • Socket fusion d20–110 and butt fusion d140–160
  • Every fitting type listed by material, with flanges, threaded transitions, clamps and supports

Specifications: pipe and fittings 20 mm to 160 mm, PN6 to PN16; threaded transitions 1/2" to 1"; clamps to 315 mm; marine flange standards GB2506 and GB573. Pricing: on request – wholesale and project quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which plastic pipe is used for ship drainage and water supply?

PE100 is used for grey water and black water drainage; PE-RT and PP-R are used for hot and cold potable and fresh water, up to 95 °C.

Are the marine piping systems class approved?

Yes. The systems hold type approval certificates from ABS, DNV, LR, BV, CCS, NK and KR and follow IMO A.753(18) and A.653(16). Certificates are supplied on request.

Which fittings are available for each material?

PE100 and PP-R: socket and butt fusion elbows, equal, reducing and 45° Y-tees, reducers, couplings, end caps, flange adaptors and plates, and threaded adaptors and unions. PE-RT: socket fusion elbows, tees, reducers, couplings, end caps, flanges and threaded fittings up to d110. PE100 also has crosses, lengthened tees and a d63 access port.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Send the piping list with system, sizes, pressure class and quantities, the vessel or project and the delivery port for a quotation.
