F&B Equipment & Supplies in Singapore

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies commercial kitchen equipment and food service packaging to restaurants, cafés, hotels, bakeries, food courts and F&B chains in Singapore and worldwide.

PANDA COLLECTIONS is a one-stop F&B equipment and supplies partner for restaurants, cafés, coffee and bubble tea chains, hotels, bakeries, food courts, cloud kitchens, canteens, caterers and central kitchens in Singapore and worldwide. The commercial kitchen range covers gas and electric cooking ranges, induction and wok ranges, jacketed and tilting kettles, braising pans, fryers, griddles, lava-stone grills, pasta and noodle cookers, bain-maries, salamanders, combi steam and convection ovens, pizza, deck and bakery ovens, dough proofers, rice steaming cabinets, rotisseries, shawarma machines, heated display cabinets, toasters, panini presses and snack machines for waffles, pancakes, crêpes, takoyaki and doughnuts.

The food service packaging range covers paper and plastic cups, lids and carriers, kraft bowls and food buckets, takeaway, burger, pizza and bakery boxes, food wraps and bags, straws, cutlery, labels and sealing film, available plain or printed with your brand.

Every kitchen model is listed with its dimensions, power or gas rating and capacity. For a quotation, send the model codes or your menu and floor plan, quantities and delivery address; equipment, packaging and other F&B supplies can be consolidated in one shipment.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What F&B equipment and supplies does PANDA COLLECTIONS provide?

Commercial kitchen equipment – gas and electric ranges, fryers, griddles, lava-stone grills, pasta cookers, bain-maries, combi and convection ovens, pizza, deck and bakery ovens, dough proofers, rice steamers, rotisseries, waffle, pancake and other snack machines – plus food service and takeaway packaging such as cups, bowls, boxes, straws, bags and labels.

Can you equip a complete restaurant or central kitchen?

Yes. Send your menu, floor plan and utilities (power and gas). We match cooking, baking and holding equipment by model, confirm power and gas ratings, and can add packaging, furniture and other supplies in one consolidated shipment.

Do the kitchen machines run on Singapore power and gas?

Each model lists its voltage and gas rating. Electric units are 220 V single-phase or 380 V three-phase; gas units are rated for LPG and natural gas. Installation should be done by licensed electrical and gas workers.

Can packaging be printed with our brand?

Yes. Cups, bowls, boxes, bags and greaseproof papers can be printed with your logo and colours; minimum order and lead time are confirmed per item.
