Corporate Flowers & Flower Delivery in Singapore

PANDA COLLECTIONS is a corporate florist and flower delivery service in Singapore. We supply 356 fresh flower designs for companies: hand bouquets, flower boxes, flower baskets, fruit hampers, grand opening flower stands and condolence flower stands (condolence wreaths). Prices start from SGD 59.90, and delivery is arranged to offices, venues, homes and funeral parlours across Singapore.

Typical corporate uses include client appreciation, staff birthdays, office reception flowers, grand opening congratulations and condolences. For bulk or multi-address orders, request a quote with recipients, dates and card messages.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer corporate flower delivery in Singapore?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS arranges corporate flower delivery to offices, venues, hospitals, homes and funeral parlours across Singapore. The delivery date and time window are confirmed with each order.

What flower arrangements are available?

356 designs across 10 types: hand bouquets, bloom boxes, flower bags, flower baskets, 99-rose bouquets, preserved flowers, bridal bouquets, fruit hampers, grand opening flower stands and condolence flower stands (condolence wreaths).

How much do corporate flowers cost?

Prices start from SGD 59.90. Each product page shows its unit price. Bulk and recurring corporate orders are quoted separately.

Can I send flowers to several addresses at once?

Yes. Send a list of recipients, addresses, dates and card messages in one quote request and the orders are coordinated together.

Can I add a card or ribbon message?

Yes. A card message is included with bouquets and boxes, and a ribbon or card with your company name can be added to grand opening and condolence stands.
