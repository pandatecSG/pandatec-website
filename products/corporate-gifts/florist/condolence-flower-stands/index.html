Condolence Wreath & Flower Stand Singapore

PANDA COLLECTIONS condolence flower stands (condolence wreaths) are sympathy flower stands for funerals, wakes and memorial services in Singapore, priced from SGD 79 to SGD 1,288. Designs use white, yellow and soft pastel gerberas, chrysanthemums, orchids and lilies, about 1.2–2.2 m tall, and are delivered to wakes and funeral parlours.

Prices are per unit. For bulk, recurring or multi-address corporate orders, request a quote with quantities, dates and delivery addresses.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a condolence wreath or flower stand in Singapore?

PANDA COLLECTIONS condolence flower stands start from SGD 79. Most stands are about 1.7–2.0 m tall (range 1.2–2.2 m) with white, yellow or pastel flowers suitable for wakes and funerals.

Can you deliver condolence flowers to a wake or funeral parlour?

Yes. Provide the wake address, the deceased's name and the condolence message. Delivery timing is confirmed when the order is placed.

Can a company name be shown on the condolence stand?

Yes. A ribbon or card with the company name and message can be included.

Are the flowers in the photo exactly what I will receive?

Fresh flowers are seasonal. If a flower in the photo is unavailable, a substitute of similar colour, style and value is used.
