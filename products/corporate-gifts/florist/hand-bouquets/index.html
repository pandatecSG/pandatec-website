Hand Bouquets Singapore

PANDA COLLECTIONS hand bouquets are fresh, hand-tied flower bouquets for companies in Singapore, priced from SGD 59.90 to SGD 460. Popular choices include red and pink roses, tulips, sunflowers, hydrangeas and eustomas. They are used for client appreciation, staff recognition, birthdays, anniversaries and congratulations, with flower delivery to offices and homes across Singapore.

Prices are per unit. For bulk, recurring or multi-address corporate orders, request a quote with quantities, dates and delivery addresses.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a hand bouquet cost in Singapore?

PANDA COLLECTIONS hand bouquets range from SGD 59.90 to SGD 460, based on the flowers and size of the arrangement. Prices are shown per unit.

Do you deliver flowers in Singapore?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS arranges flower delivery to offices, homes and venues across Singapore. The delivery date and time window are confirmed with each order.

Can I order flowers in bulk for my company?

Yes. For bulk, recurring or multi-address corporate orders, request a quote with the quantity, budget per gift, delivery addresses and dates.

Are the flowers in the photo exactly what I will receive?

Fresh flowers are seasonal. If a flower in the photo is unavailable, a substitute of similar colour, style and value is used.
