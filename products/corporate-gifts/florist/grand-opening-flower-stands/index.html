Grand Opening Flower Stand Singapore

PANDA COLLECTIONS grand opening flower stands are tall congratulatory flower stands, about 1.4–2.3 m high, priced from SGD 80 to SGD 700. They are used for grand openings, business launches, new office openings and anniversaries and are delivered to shops, malls and offices across Singapore. Single stands and two-stand sets are available.

Prices are per unit. For bulk, recurring or multi-address corporate orders, request a quote with quantities, dates and delivery addresses.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a grand opening flower stand in Singapore?

PANDA COLLECTIONS grand opening flower stands start from SGD 80 per stand. Single stands and sets of two are available; prices shown are per listing.

Can the flower stand carry a congratulatory message?

Yes. Share the company name and congratulatory message when you request a quote and it will be prepared with the stand.

Where can the grand opening stand be delivered?

Delivery is arranged to business premises across Singapore, including shopping malls, offices and industrial buildings. The delivery date and time window are confirmed with each order.

Are the flowers in the photo exactly what I will receive?

Fresh flowers are seasonal. If a flower in the photo is unavailable, a substitute of similar colour, style and value is used.
