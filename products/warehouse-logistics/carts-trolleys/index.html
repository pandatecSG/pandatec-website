Carts & Trolleys

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies carts and trolleys to supermarkets, retailers, warehouses, factories, hospitals and hotels in Singapore and worldwide: European- and American-style supermarket shopping trolleys, shopping basket trolleys, platform trolleys, chrome wire shelving and service trolleys, logistics roll cages, heavy-duty tool carts, and medical, nursing and stainless steel hospital trolleys.

Key features

  • Supermarket shopping trolleys and basket trolleys
  • Platform trolleys for cartons and bulky goods
  • Chrome wire shelving and service trolleys
  • Logistics security roll cages
  • Tool carts and medical and hospital trolleys

Specifications: standard sizes as listed per product, confirmed per order; colours, logo plates and castor options on request. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which trolley suits a supermarket?

Full-size shopping trolleys (European or American style) for weekly shoppers, and basket trolleys for convenience stores and narrow aisles.

Do you supply hospital and medical trolleys?

Yes. Emergency, nursing, medicine, anaesthesia, laundry and computer carts and stainless steel instrument trolleys are listed under Medical & Hospital Trolleys.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Request a quote with the model codes and quantities, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
