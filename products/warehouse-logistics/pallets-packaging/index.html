Pallets & Packaging

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies protective packaging and packing-line equipment to e-commerce sellers, warehouses, factories and exporters in Singapore and worldwide: air column rolls and bags, air cushion film and air pillows, bubble wrap and bubble mailers, honeycomb paper, container dunnage bags, air cushion and paper cushioning machines, and battery strapping tools with PET strap. Sizes, printing and quantities are confirmed per order.

Key features

  • Air column rolls (20–145 cm) and Q-, U- and multi-pocket air column bags with matching inflators
  • Air cushion film, air pillows, bag-in-bag air cushions and kraft paper air pillows
  • Bubble wrap rolls, bubble bags, anti-static bubble film and pearlescent bubble mailers
  • Honeycomb paper wrap and bottle sleeves as eco alternatives to plastic
  • Air cushion machines, carton perforators, battery strapping tools and PET strap

Specifications: typical values from the manufacturer, confirmed per order; custom sizes, formats and printing on request. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which protective packaging suits fragile e-commerce parcels?

Air column bags and rolls give the highest protection for bottles, electronics and cosmetics; air pillows and gourd film suit void fill and wrapping; honeycomb paper is the paper-based option.

Can packaging be made to size or printed?

Yes. Custom sizes, formats and printing are available for many items. Send the product dimensions, quantity and artwork with your enquiry.

Do you supply packing machines as well as materials?

Yes. Air column inflators, air cushion machines, carton perforating and kraft paper cushion machines, bundling machines and battery strapping tools are available, with the matching film or strap.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on size, material, quantity and delivery. Request a quote with the product codes, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
