PANDATEC supplies warehouse racking, shelving and material-handling equipment to warehouses, factories and logistics centres in Singapore. The range covers storage racks, shelving, stainless steel shelving, compact shelving, turnover boxes bins, wire containers, carts trolleys, pallets packaging. Each enquiry starts with the intended application and the requirements that matter to your organisation. You can browse a subcategory, select relevant products for a quote, or describe a requirement that is not yet listed.

For a useful sourcing brief, include quantities, dimensions, materials, preferred finishes and the delivery location where these are relevant. Drawings, reference photographs and a bill of quantities can help explain a project. Identify any mandatory standards or documentation needed for your intended use so these can be reviewed for the specific product.

PANDATEC coordinates sourcing, procurement, supplier communication, quality control and logistics. Customisation, delivery timing, installation support and any required product evidence are confirmed individually. The catalogue should be used as a starting point for an enquiry; it does not establish stock availability or certify suitability. If several product categories are involved, include the overall project scope so consolidation and delivery requirements can be considered together.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose between heavy-duty and medium-duty racks?

Describe the goods, weights, dimensions, storage positions and handling method. Have your responsible technical team define the required configuration and acceptance criteria. Category labels alone do not establish that a rack is suitable for the application.

What load capacity do your racks support?

Capacity depends on the offered rack and its configuration. Request documented ratings for that configuration and any conditions affecting their use. No generic load rating is assigned to sample products on this site.

Do you provide rack installation in Singapore?

State the proposed equipment, site layout and installation work required. Delivery and selected installation support can be discussed, but the scope, qualified parties and site requirements must be confirmed for the project.
