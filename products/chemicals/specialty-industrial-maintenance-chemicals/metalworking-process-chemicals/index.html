Metalworking Fluids & Process Chemicals

Metalworking fluids cool and lubricate the cutting zone, flush away chips and protect parts from rust. PANDA COLLECTIONS sources soluble oils, semi-synthetic and synthetic coolants, neat cutting and grinding oils, rust preventives, process cleaners and surface treatment chemicals such as phosphating and passivation products for CNC machining, stamping and metal finishing.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between soluble oil and synthetic coolant?

Soluble oil forms a milky emulsion with good lubrication for general machining. Synthetic coolants contain no mineral oil, give clear solutions with strong cooling and cleanliness, and suit grinding and high-speed operations.

How often should coolant concentration be checked?

Concentration is usually checked daily or weekly with a refractometer, along with pH, so that tool life, rust protection and bacterial control stay within the supplier's recommended range.

Do you show chemical prices on the website?

No. Chemical prices move with feedstock markets and depend on grade, purity, packaging, quantity, Incoterms and destination. Send your requirement and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
