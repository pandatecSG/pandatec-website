PANDA COLLECTIONS, the curated procurement brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD. (Singapore, est. 2015), supports B2B buyers with chemical sourcing for manufacturing, packaging, construction, agriculture, water treatment, electronics and industrial maintenance. The range is organised in ten groups: plastic raw materials and synthetic resins, industrial solvents and basic chemicals, petrochemical raw materials, fertilizers and agrochemicals, coatings, resins and adhesive raw materials, specialty chemicals and industrial consumables, polyurethane raw materials and systems, superabsorbent polymers, aroma chemicals, and battery materials.

A useful chemical enquiry names the product and, where possible, its CAS number, grade, purity or key specification, application, quantity and unit (kg, MT, drums, IBC or containers), packaging, destination port or delivery address, required delivery date, preferred Incoterms and the documents you need, such as SDS, TDS or COA.

PANDA COLLECTIONS identifies qualified manufacturers and supply options, checks documents and product identity, coordinates samples, quality checks and supplier communication, and arranges logistics to Singapore or onward export. Product availability, origin, specification, dangerous-goods transport, import permits and lead time are confirmed for each enquiry; the catalogue is a starting point for sourcing, not a statement of stock.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What chemicals can PANDA COLLECTIONS source?

PANDA COLLECTIONS reviews B2B sourcing enquiries for commodity and engineering plastics (PE, PP, PVC, PET, ABS), industrial solvents (methanol, ethanol, acetone, toluene, xylene, ethyl acetate), petrochemical feedstocks (propylene, ethane, ammonia, aromatics), fertilizers (urea, ammonium sulphate, DAP, potassium chloride), coating and adhesive raw materials (epoxy, polyurethane, acrylic resins, pigments) and specialty chemicals such as water-treatment, cleaning, metalworking and electronic chemicals.

Do you supply chemicals outside Singapore?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS is based in Singapore and can coordinate supply to Singapore and to buyers in Southeast Asia and other markets, subject to the product classification, dangerous-goods rules, import permits and registration requirements of the destination country.

What documents come with a chemical quotation?

Depending on the product and manufacturer, a quotation can be supported by a Safety Data Sheet (SDS), Technical Data Sheet (TDS), Certificate of Analysis (COA), product specification, packing details and certificate of origin. Required documents should be stated in the enquiry.

Do you show chemical prices on the website?

No. Chemical prices move with feedstock markets and depend on grade, purity, packaging, quantity, Incoterms and destination. Send your requirement and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
