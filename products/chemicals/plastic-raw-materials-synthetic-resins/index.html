Plastic Raw Materials & Synthetic Resins

PANDA COLLECTIONS sources plastic raw materials and synthetic resins for packaging, automotive components, construction materials, electrical and electronic products, home appliances and consumer goods. Enquiries cover commodity polymers – polyethylene (PE), polypropylene (PP), polyvinyl chloride (PVC), polyethylene terephthalate (PET) and polystyrene (PS) – as well as ABS, engineering plastics and compounded grades, supplied as pellets, granules or powder for buyers in Singapore and overseas.

Applications

  • Packaging: film, bottles, caps, containers and woven bags
  • Automotive interior and exterior parts
  • Construction: pipes, profiles, cables and sheets
  • Electrical, electronic and home-appliance housings
  • Consumer goods, toys and household products

What to include in your RFQ

  • Polymer type and grade, or a reference grade name
  • Processing method: injection moulding, extrusion, blow moulding, film or fibre
  • Melt flow index (MFI), density, K-value or intrinsic viscosity where relevant
  • Colour, additives or food-contact / RoHS requirements
  • Quantity (MT), packaging (25 kg bags, jumbo bags), destination and Incoterms

Documents: TDS, COA per lot and SDS where issued; food-contact or RoHS / REACH declarations confirmed per grade. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What information is needed to quote plastic resin?

Provide the polymer type, grade or reference grade, processing method, key properties such as MFI and density, quantity, packaging, destination and any TDS, COA or compliance documents required.

Do plastic resin grades differ by application?

Yes. Injection moulding, film extrusion, blow moulding, pipe extrusion and fibre spinning need different melt flow, density and additive packages, so the grade must be matched to the process and end use.

Do you show chemical prices on the website?

No. Chemical prices move with feedstock markets and depend on grade, purity, packaging, quantity, Incoterms and destination. Send your requirement and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
