Emollients, Silicones & Waxes

Esters, hydrocarbons, silicones and waxes that set the skin feel of creams, lotions, sunscreens, make-up, hair serums and ointments: caprylic/capric triglyceride, isopropyl myristate, ethylhexyl palmitate, isononyl isononanoate, C12-15 alkyl benzoate, white mineral oil, isohexadecane, hydrogenated polydecene, dimethicone and cyclosiloxanes, PEG-12 dimethicone, white petrolatum and beeswax. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies cosmetic, personal care and household-product manufacturers in Singapore and worldwide, in small packs, drums and bulk quantities.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which emollient gives a light, non-greasy feel?

Light esters such as isononyl isononanoate and C12-15 alkyl benzoate, isohexadecane, cyclosiloxanes and low-viscosity dimethicone give a fast-spreading, non-greasy finish.

What is GTCC used for?

Caprylic/capric triglyceride is a stable, light emollient and solvent used in creams, lotions, sunscreens, make-up, massage oils and as a carrier for oil-soluble actives.

How do I get a quotation for emollients, silicones & waxes?

Prices are not published. Send PANDA COLLECTIONS the product names or codes, grade, quantity, packaging and delivery country; small-pack, wholesale and long-term supply quotations are prepared per enquiry.

Do you show cosmetic raw material prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on grade, purity, pack size, quantity, Incoterms and destination and move with raw material markets. Send your requirement and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
