Surfactants & Cleansing Agents

Primary and secondary surfactants for facial cleansers, shampoos, body wash, hand wash, toothpaste and household detergents: mild amino-acid surfactants (glutamates, sarcosinates, glycinates, apple amino acids), sodium cocoyl isethionate, sulfate surfactants (SLES and SLS), amphoteric betaines, alkyl polyglucoside and non-ionic ethoxylates and foam boosters. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies cosmetic, personal care and household-product manufacturers in Singapore and worldwide, in small packs, drums and bulk quantities.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which surfactants are milder than SLES and SLS?

Amino-acid surfactants such as sodium lauroyl glutamate, sodium cocoyl glutamate, potassium cocoyl glycinate and sodium lauroyl sarcosinate, together with sodium cocoyl isethionate, alkyl polyglucoside and betaines, are used for mild, sulfate-free cleansers.

Why is a betaine added to a shampoo?

Amphoteric betaines such as cocamidopropyl betaine boost and stabilise foam, help build viscosity with salt and reduce the irritation of anionic surfactants.
