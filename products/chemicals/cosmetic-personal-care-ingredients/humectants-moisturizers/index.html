FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between high and low molecular weight hyaluronic acid?

High molecular weight sodium hyaluronate forms a moisture-holding film on the skin surface; low molecular weight and oligo grades are smaller molecules used for a lighter feel and deeper-acting hydration. Multi-weight blends combine both.

Is butylene glycol or propylene glycol better for skincare?

Both are humectants and solvents. 1,3-butylene glycol is often preferred in premium skincare for its lighter skin feel and preservative-boosting effect; propylene glycol is a cost-effective humectant and carrier.
