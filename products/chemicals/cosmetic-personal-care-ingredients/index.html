Cosmetic & Personal Care Raw Materials

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies cosmetic and personal care raw materials to skincare, haircare, toiletry and household-product manufacturers, OEM/ODM fillers and formulation labs in Singapore and worldwide. The range covers amino-acid and sulfate surfactants, hyaluronic acid and other humectants, emulsifiers, thickeners and rheology modifiers, cosmetic preservatives, whitening, anti-ageing and repair actives, botanical extracts, plant oils and butters, and emollients, silicones and waxes, in drum, bag and small-pack quantities.

Applications

  • Skincare: serums, toners, creams, lotions, masks and sunscreens
  • Haircare: shampoos, conditioners, hair masks and scalp care
  • Personal wash: facial cleansers, body wash, hand wash, soaps and syndet bars
  • Baby care, oral care and men's grooming
  • Household and institutional cleaning products

What to include in your RFQ

  • INCI name, CAS number or your current supplier's grade
  • Purity, active content or key specification
  • Intended product type and target markets
  • Quantity: sample, small pack, drum or pallet
  • Delivery country and preferred Incoterms

Documents: Certificate of Analysis (COA) per batch, Safety Data Sheet (SDS) and Technical Data Sheet (TDS); specification sheets and regulatory statements (for example allergen, origin or BSE/TSE statements) on request where issued by the manufacturer. Regulatory: cosmetic products sold in Singapore must comply with the ASEAN Cosmetic Directive administered by HSA; ingredient limits are checked per target market. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which cosmetic raw materials does PANDA COLLECTIONS supply?

Amino-acid and sulfate surfactants and betaines, hyaluronic acid and other humectants, emulsifiers, thickeners and carbomer, cosmetic preservatives, whitening, anti-ageing and repair actives, botanical extracts, plant oils, butters and squalane, and emollients, silicones and waxes – 161 catalogued ingredients, with others sourced to your INCI list.

Can you supply small quantities for product development?

Yes. Many actives and specialty ingredients are packed in 100 g to 1 kg bottles or bags, and samples or small packs can be arranged before larger orders.

Which documents come with cosmetic raw materials?

A COA per batch, an SDS and a TDS, plus specification sheets and regulatory statements on request where issued by the manufacturer.

Do the ingredients meet Singapore cosmetic rules?

Cosmetic products in Singapore are regulated by HSA under the ASEAN Cosmetic Directive. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies the technical and regulatory documents; the product owner confirms ingredient limits and claims for each market.

How do I get a quotation?

Send the INCI names or product codes, grade, quantity, packaging and delivery country. Prices are quoted on request.
