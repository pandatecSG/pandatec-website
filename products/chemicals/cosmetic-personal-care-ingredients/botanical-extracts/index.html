Botanical Extracts

Water- and glycol-based plant extracts for toners, serums, creams, masks, shampoos and scalp care, covering soothing (centella, purslane, chamomile, aloe), astringent (witch hazel), brightening (licorice), antioxidant (rosemary, ginseng, rhodiola), haircare (polygonum multiflorum, eclipta, platycladus, ginger, soapnut) and marine extracts, plus snail secretion filtrate. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies cosmetic, personal care and household-product manufacturers in Singapore and worldwide, in small packs, drums and bulk quantities.

How PANDA COLLECTIONS supports you

  • Supplier identification and qualification

  • Small packs, samples and pre-shipment quality checks on request

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How are botanical extracts supplied?

Most extracts are liquid extracts in water, glycerin or glycols, supplied in 18–25 kg drums with a COA and specification; carrier, preservative and concentration are confirmed per batch.

Which extracts are used for sensitive skin?

Centella asiatica, purslane, chamomile, aloe and licorice extracts are widely used in soothing and sensitive-skin formulas.

How do I get a quotation for botanical extracts?

Prices are not published. Send PANDA COLLECTIONS the product names or codes, grade, quantity, packaging and delivery country; small-pack, wholesale and long-term supply quotations are prepared per enquiry.

Do you show cosmetic raw material prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on grade, purity, pack size, quantity, Incoterms and destination and move with raw material markets. Send your requirement and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
