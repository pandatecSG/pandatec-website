Cosmetic Active Ingredients

Functional actives for brightening, anti-ageing, soothing and barrier-repair products: vitamins and vitamin derivatives (niacinamide, ascorbyl glucoside, ethyl ascorbic acid, sodium ascorbyl phosphate, retinyl palmitate, pyridoxine), arbutin, tranexamic acid, phenylethyl resorcinol and potassium methoxysalicylate, peptides, ectoin, ergothioneine, coenzyme Q10, ferment lysates, polysaccharides, allantoin, bisabolol and glycyrrhizate, AHAs and enzymes. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies cosmetic, personal care and household-product manufacturers in Singapore and worldwide, in small packs, drums and bulk quantities.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which ingredients are used for skin brightening?

Common brightening actives include niacinamide, alpha-arbutin, tranexamic acid, 3-O-ethyl ascorbic acid, ascorbyl glucoside, phenylethyl resorcinol, potassium methoxysalicylate and nonapeptide-1, used within the limits allowed in each market.

Can small quantities of actives be supplied?

Yes. Many actives are packed in 1 kg bottles or bags; small-pack and sample requests are confirmed per product.

How do I get a quotation for cosmetic active ingredients?

Prices are not published. Send PANDA COLLECTIONS the product names or codes, grade, quantity, packaging and delivery country; small-pack, wholesale and long-term supply quotations are prepared per enquiry.

Do you show cosmetic raw material prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on grade, purity, pack size, quantity, Incoterms and destination and move with raw material markets. Send your requirement and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
