BUYING GUIDE

How to choose food wraps & paper bags

When comparing food wraps & paper bags for a food business, check:

  • Sheet or bag size
  • Grease and oil resistance
  • Printed pattern and colours
  • Quantity per pack and carton

Include sizes, quantities, printing artwork and the delivery location in your enquiry.

What should I check when buying food wraps & paper bags?

Check sheet or bag size, grease and oil resistance, printed pattern and colours, quantity per pack and carton. Confirm the details for each item on its product page or in the quotation.

Which businesses buy food wraps & paper bags?

Cafés and coffee chains, bubble tea and juice bars, restaurants and cloud kitchens, bakeries, hotels, caterers and canteens.

How do I get a quotation for food wraps & paper bags in Singapore?

Prices are not published. Send PANDA COLLECTIONS the product codes or sizes, quantities, printing artwork and delivery address.

Can the packaging be printed with our logo?

Yes. Most cups, bowls, boxes, bags and greaseproof papers can be printed with your logo and colours; minimum order, print method and lead time are confirmed in the quotation.

Is the packaging suitable for food contact?

The range is made for food service. Ask for the food-contact declarations and test reports required in your market with the quotation, and confirm suitability for hot, oily or frozen food for your specific use.
