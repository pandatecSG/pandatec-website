BUYING GUIDE

How to choose takeaway food boxes

When comparing takeaway food boxes for a food business, check:

  • Volume or inside dimensions for the portion
  • Grease, moisture and heat resistance
  • Fold-closed, two-piece or windowed lid
  • Printing, stacking and carton quantity

Include sizes, quantities, printing artwork and the delivery location in your enquiry.

What should I check when buying takeaway food boxes?

Check volume or inside dimensions for the portion, grease, moisture and heat resistance, fold-closed, two-piece or windowed lid, printing, stacking and carton quantity. Confirm the details for each item on its product page or in the quotation.

Which businesses buy takeaway food boxes?

Cafés and coffee chains, bubble tea and juice bars, restaurants and cloud kitchens, bakeries, hotels, caterers and canteens.

Can the packaging be printed with our logo?

Yes. Most cups, bowls, boxes, bags and greaseproof papers can be printed with your logo and colours; minimum order, print method and lead time are confirmed in the quotation.

Is the packaging suitable for food contact?

The range is made for food service. Ask for the food-contact declarations and test reports required in your market with the quotation, and confirm suitability for hot, oily or frozen food for your specific use.
