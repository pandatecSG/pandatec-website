Food Service Packaging Supplier in Singapore

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies disposable cups, lids, bowls, takeaway boxes, bakery boxes and greaseproof paper to cafés, restaurants, bakeries and F&B chains in Singapore and worldwide.

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies food service and takeaway packaging to cafés, coffee and bubble tea chains, restaurants, cloud kitchens, bakeries, hotels, caterers and canteens in Singapore and worldwide. The range covers paper hot drink cups from 8 to 22 oz, clear PET and PP cold cups and lids, cup carriers, kraft salad bowls, soup and noodle buckets, folding lunch boxes, octagonal and sushi boxes, burger, hot dog, fries and pizza boxes, cake, mousse and egg tart boxes, greaseproof paper, carrier and insulated bags, paper, PLA, PP and bamboo-fibre straws, cutlery, cup sleeves, labels, stickers, receipt rolls and cup sealing film.

Most items are available plain or printed with your logo, colours and artwork, with special finishes such as hot stamping, embossing, spot UV and lamination for cups and gift boxes. For a quotation, include the item, size or capacity, quantity, printing artwork and delivery address; sample checks are available before larger orders.

PANDA COLLECTIONS coordinates sourcing, printing, quality control and logistics, and can consolidate packaging with other F&B supplies such as furniture, kitchen equipment and uniforms in one shipment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which food service packaging does PANDA COLLECTIONS supply?

Paper hot cups and clear cold cups with lids and carriers, kraft salad bowls, soup and noodle buckets, takeaway, burger, hot dog, fries, sushi and pizza boxes, cake, mousse and egg tart boxes, and greaseproof paper and bags.

Can packaging be printed with our brand?

Yes. Cups, bowls, boxes, bags and greaseproof papers can be printed with your logo and colours; minimum order and lead time are confirmed per item.

Do you supply cafés and restaurants in Singapore?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies cafés, bubble tea shops, restaurants, cloud kitchens, bakeries, hotels and caterers in Singapore and worldwide, with consolidated delivery.

How do I get a quotation?

Send the product codes or sizes, quantities, printing artwork and the delivery address; prices are quoted by quantity.
