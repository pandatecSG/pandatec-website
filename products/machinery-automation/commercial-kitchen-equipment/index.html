BUYING GUIDE

How to choose commercial kitchen equipment

When comparing commercial kitchen equipment for a business, check:

  • Menu and output per hour
  • Gas or electric supply
  • Kitchen layout and ventilation
  • Cleaning and hygiene

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies commercial kitchen equipment to restaurants, central kitchens, canteens and hotels and bakeries in Singapore and worldwide. Include quantities, specifications and the delivery location in your enquiry.

What should I check when buying commercial kitchen equipment?

Check menu and output per hour, gas or electric supply, kitchen layout and ventilation and cleaning and hygiene. Confirm the details for each model on its product page or in the quotation.

Which businesses buy commercial kitchen equipment?

Typical buyers include restaurants, central kitchens, canteens and hotels and bakeries.
