PANDATEC supplies light machinery, workshop machines and automation equipment sourced from manufacturers across Asia to businesses in Singapore. The range covers cutting sawing machines, drilling machines, sanding grinding machines, welding machines, industrial air circulation, automation robotics, commercial kitchen equipment, custom machinery. Each enquiry starts with the intended application and the requirements that matter to your organisation. You can browse a subcategory, select relevant products for a quote, or describe a requirement that is not yet listed.

For a useful sourcing brief, include quantities, dimensions, materials, preferred finishes and the delivery location where these are relevant. Drawings, reference photographs and a bill of quantities can help explain a project. Identify any mandatory standards or documentation needed for your intended use so these can be reviewed for the specific product.

PANDATEC coordinates sourcing, procurement, supplier communication, quality control and logistics. Customisation, delivery timing, installation support and any required product evidence are confirmed individually. The catalogue should be used as a starting point for an enquiry; it does not establish stock availability or certify suitability. If several product categories are involved, include the overall project scope so consolidation and delivery requirements can be considered together.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide machine specifications and voltage for Singapore (230V)?

Request the manufacturer documentation for the exact configuration being offered. Share your site power supply, phase, frequency and connection requirements for review by a qualified person. Do not assume every machine uses 230V or that a plug change establishes compatibility.

Can you source custom machinery?

Send an authorised drawing or functional brief describing the task, workpiece, operating environment and quantity. A supplier can assess feasibility; engineering work, drawings, testing and any approval requirements must be agreed separately.

Can inspections be arranged before shipment?

Discuss the inspection scope before placing the order. Identify the approved specification, documents, checks and reporting needed. Availability and responsibilities require agreement; an inspection is not a blanket performance guarantee or certification.

Is after-sales support available?

Ask what support, warranty terms, spare parts and documentation can be confirmed for the specific offered machine. Arrangements vary by supplier and product. This draft does not promise an in-house repair service, commissioning or a universal warranty.
