PANDA COLLECTIONS, the procurement brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD., supports pharmaceutical, biopharmaceutical, CDMO, laboratory and sterile manufacturing procurement in Singapore. The range covers GMP garments and sterile apparel, sterile and non-sterile gloves, disinfectants and cleaning agents, wipes, mops and cleaning systems, sterile packaging and single-use components, filtration and process consumables, laboratory and QC supplies, pharmaceutical packaging components, hygienic stainless steel equipment, cleanroom furniture and pass-throughs, temperature-controlled storage and logistics, and waste and biohazard handling.

To select the right item, state whether it is product-contact or non-contact, sterile or non-sterile, the sterilisation method, material compatibility, batch traceability, packaging integrity and the quality documents required.

Claims such as GMP compliance, sterility, USP / EP compliance or suitability for direct drug contact are only made with documents for the specific product and batch; PANDA COLLECTIONS reviews these documents with the supplier before supply.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What pharmaceutical and GMP supplies does PANDA COLLECTIONS source?

GMP and sterile garments, sterile and non-sterile gloves, disinfectants and cleaning agents, low-lint and sterile wipes, mops, sterile packaging and single-use components, filtration, laboratory and QC supplies, pharmaceutical packaging components, hygienic stainless steel equipment, cold-chain packaging and biohazard waste supplies.

Are the products GMP compliant or sterile?

This depends on the specific product and batch. Sterility, GMP, USP / EP or drug-contact suitability is only stated with documents for the offered item, such as certificates of analysis, sterility and validation reports.

What information is needed for a pharmaceutical supply quote?

Item and size, quantity, contact status, sterile or non-sterile, sterilisation method, material compatibility, batch traceability, documents required and delivery schedule.

Do you supply CDMOs and laboratories?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS supports pharmaceutical plants, biotechnology companies, CDMOs, laboratories and QC teams in Singapore and international markets.
