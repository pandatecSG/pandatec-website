PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies office, system, hospitality, school and outdoor furniture to businesses and institutions in Singapore and worldwide, including custom and built-in carpentry. The range covers system furniture, office chairs, acoustic pods, reception lounge, sofas & lounge chairs, dining furniture, school furniture, outdoor furniture, hotel accommodation, custom carpentry. Each enquiry starts with the intended application and the requirements that matter to your organisation. You can browse a subcategory, select relevant products for a quote, or describe a requirement that is not yet listed.

For a useful sourcing brief, include quantities, dimensions, materials, preferred finishes and the delivery location where these are relevant. Drawings, reference photographs and a bill of quantities can help explain a project. Identify any mandatory standards or documentation needed for your intended use so these can be reviewed for the specific product.

PANDA COLLECTIONS coordinates sourcing, procurement, supplier communication, quality control and logistics. Customisation, delivery timing, installation support and any required product evidence are confirmed individually. The catalogue should be used as a starting point for an enquiry; it does not establish stock availability or certify suitability. If several product categories are involved, include the overall project scope so consolidation and delivery requirements can be considered together.
