PANDATEC supplies office, system, hospitality, school and outdoor furniture to businesses and institutions in Singapore, including custom and built-in carpentry. The range covers system furniture, office chairs, acoustic pods, reception lounge, luxury collection, dining furniture, school furniture, outdoor furniture, hotel accommodation, custom carpentry. Each enquiry starts with the intended application and the requirements that matter to your organisation. You can browse a subcategory, select relevant products for a quote, or describe a requirement that is not yet listed.

For a useful sourcing brief, include quantities, dimensions, materials, preferred finishes and the delivery location where these are relevant. Drawings, reference photographs and a bill of quantities can help explain a project. Identify any mandatory standards or documentation needed for your intended use so these can be reviewed for the specific product.

PANDATEC coordinates sourcing, procurement, supplier communication, quality control and logistics. Customisation, delivery timing, installation support and any required product evidence are confirmed individually. The catalogue should be used as a starting point for an enquiry; it does not establish stock availability or certify suitability. If several product categories are involved, include the overall project scope so consolidation and delivery requirements can be considered together.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you supply BIFMA-certified chairs?

Tell us which requirement your project specifies. Certification is product-specific; request the relevant evidence for the exact chair being offered. The sample catalogue makes no certification claim.

Can furniture be customised to our office layout?

Share an authorised layout, dimensions, quantities and preferred finishes. Customisation can be discussed with suppliers, with feasibility, drawings, quantities and commercial terms confirmed for the proposed furniture.

What is the lead time for office furniture?

Timing depends on the selected product, quantity, finish, supplier readiness and delivery arrangements. Ask for the proposed schedule in your quotation; the website does not confirm stock or a standard lead time.

Do you provide delivery and installation in Singapore?

PANDATEC coordinates local delivery and selected installation support. State the site access and work required so that delivery, assembly and any separate installation scope can be confirmed.
