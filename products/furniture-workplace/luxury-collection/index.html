Sofas & Lounge Chairs

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies commercial sofas, lounge chairs and armchairs for hotel lobbies, executive offices, showrooms, clubhouses and residential projects in Singapore. The range covers top-grain leather and fabric sofas, channel-tufted and Chesterfield styles, swivel and tub armchairs, Modern Oriental solid-wood sets, and matching coffee tables, side tables and accent pieces, with sizes, upholstery and finishes confirmed per project.

Key features

  • Sofas: 1-, 2- and 3-seater, corner and chaise configurations in top-grain leather or fabric
  • Lounge chairs: armchairs, swivel chairs, tub and barrel chairs, sling and safari chairs
  • Coffee & side tables: marble, solid wood, forged iron and leather-top designs
  • Modern Oriental solid-wood sofa sets with matching chairs, tables and TV cabinets
  • Custom upholstery, colour and size options for hospitality and office fit-outs

Materials & documents: upholstery material, frame, foam density and finish stated per product; fabric and leather samples available on request. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What sofas and lounge chairs does PANDA COLLECTIONS supply?

Leather and fabric sofas, corner sofas, armchairs, swivel and tub chairs, Modern Oriental solid-wood sets, and matching coffee and side tables for hotels, offices, showrooms and residences in Singapore.

Can the upholstery, colour or size be customised?

Yes. Most models can be made in other leathers, fabrics, colours and seat widths. Send the model code and your requirement, and the options and lead time are confirmed in the quotation.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on model, upholstery, quantity and delivery. Request a quote with the product codes, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
