Coffee & Side Tables

Coffee tables, side tables and nesting tables to complete sofa and lounge chair settings in hotels, offices, showrooms and residences in Singapore. Materials include fluted marble, solid timber, forged iron, aluminium and leather tops, in rectangular, round, tray-top, trunk-style and two-tier designs.

Key features

  • Coffee, side, nesting and pedestal tables
  • Marble, solid timber, forged iron, aluminium and leather-top finishes
  • Two-tier, tray-top, drawer and trunk-style designs
  • Matching tables for the sofa and lounge chair collections
  • Custom sizes and finishes for project orders

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a coffee table size for a sofa?

A coffee table is usually about two-thirds of the sofa length, with around 40–45 cm clearance between table and sofa. Send the sofa model and room size for a suggestion.

Can tables be ordered in another size or finish?

Yes. Most models can be made in other sizes, timber finishes or stone tops. The options and lead time are confirmed in the quotation.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on model, material, quantity and delivery. Request a quote with the product codes, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
