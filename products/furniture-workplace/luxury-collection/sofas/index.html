FURNITURE & WORKPLACE · SOFAS & LOUNGE CHAIRS

Sofas

Commercial sofas for hotel lobbies, executive offices, VIP rooms, showrooms and residences in Singapore. The range includes top-grain leather 3-seater sofas, corner sofas with chaise, classic Chesterfield and club sofas, channel-tufted and low-profile modern sofas, and Modern Oriental solid-wood sofas with matching chairs and tables.

Key features

  • Top-grain cowhide leather and commercial fabric upholstery
  • 3-seater, 2-seater, corner and chaise configurations
  • Chesterfield, club, channel-tufted, low-profile and Art Deco styles
  • Solid-wood, metal and forged-iron bases and legs
  • Custom colours, sizes and upholstery for project orders

Materials & documents: upholstery, frame, foam and dimensions stated per product; leather and fabric samples on request. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Which sofa suits a hotel lobby or office reception?

Top-grain leather sofas with fixed cushions are durable and easy to clean for high-traffic areas; channel-tufted or Chesterfield styles suit premium lounges and VIP rooms.

Can I order a sofa in a different size or leather?

Yes. Most models can be made in other widths, leathers or fabrics. The options and lead time are confirmed in the quotation.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on model, upholstery, quantity and delivery. Request a quote with the product codes, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
