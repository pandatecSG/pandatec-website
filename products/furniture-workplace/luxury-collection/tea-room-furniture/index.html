Tea Tables & Tea Room Furniture

Tea room furniture for hotels, clubhouses, tea houses, executive offices and villas: large tea tables with integrated tea trays and storage, solid wood and light luxury tea tables with stone tops, kung fu tea tables for lounges, tea carts, antique (bogu) display shelves, and matching host chairs, guest chairs, benches and stools.

Key features

  • Tea tables from 1200 to 3500 mm
  • Solid wood, sintered stone and luxury stone finishes
  • Host and guest tea chairs, benches and stools
  • Tea carts and antique display shelves

Specifications: typical values, confirmed per order; sizes, wood colours and stone panels to order. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.
