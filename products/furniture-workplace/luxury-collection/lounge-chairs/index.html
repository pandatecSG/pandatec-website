Lounge Chairs

Lounge chairs and armchairs for hotel rooms and lobbies, executive offices, meeting lounges, showrooms and residences in Singapore. The range covers leather and fabric armchairs, swivel and tub chairs, barrel chairs, sling and safari chairs, cantilever designs and Modern Oriental solid-wood guest chairs.

Key features

  • Leather, fabric and mixed-upholstery armchairs and leisure chairs
  • Swivel, tub, barrel, sling, safari and cantilever designs
  • Solid-wood, steel, brass-finish and forged-iron frames
  • Pairs with sofas and coffee tables in the same collection
  • Custom upholstery and colour for project orders

Materials & documents: upholstery, frame and dimensions stated per product; leather and fabric samples on request. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a lounge chair and an armchair?

Both are upholstered single seats; lounge chairs usually have a deeper, more reclined seat for relaxing, while armchairs sit more upright and suit guest seating and meeting lounges.

Can lounge chairs be matched with a sofa?

Yes. Many chairs come from the same collections as the sofas and coffee tables, so a full lounge set can be quoted together.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Prices depend on model, upholstery, quantity and delivery. Request a quote with the product codes, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
