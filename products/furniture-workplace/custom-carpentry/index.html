Custom Carpentry

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies made-to-measure carpentry for hotels, serviced apartments, residential developers, fit-out contractors and offices in Singapore and worldwide: powder-coated cabinet and wardrobe doors, interior and concealed doors with matching architraves, arc wall panels and curved profiles, and display, storage and study cabinets in coordinated styles and colours.

Key features

  • Powder-coated MDF or OSB doors and panels, made to measure
  • Coordinated wardrobe doors, interior doors and wall panels
  • Concealed doors flush with wall panels
  • Curved panels and columns from R37 to R300
  • Display cabinets and home-office wall units

Specifications: made to measure; styles, colours, handles and sizes confirmed per order. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a powder-coated cabinet door?

An MDF or OSB door panel finished by electrostatic powder coating and cured at about 150 °C. The solid powder coats all six edges without joints, giving a moisture-, scratch- and wear-resistant surface with no solvent-based paint.

Can doors, wardrobes and wall panels be matched?

Yes. Cabinet doors, interior doors and wall panels are offered in the same styles and colours so a room can be finished as one set.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Send drawings or room sizes, the style codes and the delivery location, and PANDA COLLECTIONS will reply with a customised quotation.
