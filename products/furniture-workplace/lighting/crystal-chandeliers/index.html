Crystal Chandeliers

Crystal chandeliers for hotel lobbies, ballrooms, restaurants, villas and residences: classic clear and tinted glass-arm chandeliers, multi-tier and grand lobby pieces up to 144 lights, crystal-draped and shaded designs, coloured and pastel glass, Maria Theresa chandeliers in six glass colours, cast-alloy, modern tiered and spiral, and gold empire-style chandeliers. Bespoke tiered, drum, halo, cascade, lantern, candle, shaded-ring and wrought-iron chandeliers are made to the space.

Key features

  • 6 to 144 lights; up to Ø2,500 × H4,200 mm
  • Clear, amber, smoke, coloured and pastel glass
  • Maria Theresa, glass-arm, empire and modern styles
  • Fabric-shade and hurricane-glass options
  • Matching wall lamps for many ranges

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Maria Theresa chandelier?

A classic design with flat, sweeping glass arms and cascading crystal drops, named after the 18th-century Austrian empress; it suits ballrooms, dining rooms and hotel lobbies.

Which chandelier suits a double-height lobby?

Grand lobby, multi-tier Maria Theresa or tall empire and spiral models up to H4,200 mm. Send the ceiling height and space size for a recommendation.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Request a quote with the model codes and quantities.
