Wall Lamps & Sconces

Wall lamps and sconces to match the chandelier ranges: Maria Theresa crystal wall lamps in five glass finishes, gold-finish crystal sconces, antique-bronze cast-metal sconces with amber, glass, white or jade-tone shades, and glass-arm crystal sconces in clear, amber, smoke, blue, pastel and red. Tall vertical crystal wall lights for corridors and lift lobbies are matched to crystal ceiling fixtures.

Key features

  • 1 to 5 lights
  • Gold-finish and antique-bronze cast metal
  • Glass-arm crystal sconces in several colours
  • Shaded and unshaded versions

Specifications: sizes and finishes confirmed per order. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.
