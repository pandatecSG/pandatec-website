Table & Floor Lamps

Table and floor lamps for hotel guest rooms, suites, lounges and lobbies: table lamps with ceramic, glass and metal bases and fabric shades, pleated, column, twin-shade and arc floor lamps, crystal-prism and cut-crystal lamps, glowing glass column feature lamps, and candelabra-style crystal table and floor lamps in clear, gold, ruby and green glass.

Key features

  • Ceramic, glass, crystal and metal bases
  • Drum, empire, square and pleated fabric shades
  • Single-shade, twin-shade and arc floor lamps
  • Candelabra crystal lamps up to H2,220 mm

Specifications: typical values, confirmed per order. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.
