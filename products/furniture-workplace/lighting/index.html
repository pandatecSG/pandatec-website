Lighting

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies decorative and hospitality lighting for hotels, restaurants, banquet halls, clubhouses, villas, offices and showrooms in Singapore and worldwide: glass-arm, Maria Theresa, empire and modern crystal chandeliers, bespoke crystal ceiling fixtures made to the ceiling plan, raindrop, Oriental and contemporary pendant lights, hand-blown art glass installations, crystal and cast-metal wall sconces, table and floor lamps, recessed LED spotlights and downlights, and garden post lamps and bollards.

Key features

  • Crystal chandeliers from 6 to 144 lights, and bespoke fixtures made to the ceiling plan
  • Crystal ceiling fixtures for ballrooms, lobbies and corridors
  • Pendant lights, art glass installations and wall sconces
  • Hotel table and floor lamps
  • LED spotlights and downlights; garden post lamps and bollards

Specifications: typical values from the manufacturer, confirmed per order; custom sizes, light counts, glass colours and finishes on request. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a chandelier size for a room?

As a guide, add the room length and width in metres and use the total in centimetres as the chandelier diameter; for double-height lobbies, choose a taller multi-tier or tower model. Send the room size and ceiling height for a recommendation.

Can chandeliers be made to a project size?

Yes. Light counts, sizes, glass colours and finishes can be adapted for hotel and residential projects.

Do you show prices on the website?
