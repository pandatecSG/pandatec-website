BUYING GUIDE

How to choose dining chairs

When comparing dining chairs for a business, check:

  • Seat height about 43–46 cm for 72–76 cm tables
  • Frame strength and stackability
  • Upholstery or PP shell for cleaning
  • Armchair clearance under the table

Check seat height about 43–46 cm for 72–76 cm tables, frame strength and stackability, upholstery or PP shell for cleaning and armchair clearance under the table. Confirm the details for each model on its product page or in the quotation.

Which businesses buy dining chairs?

Typical buyers include restaurants and cafés, hotels, canteens and office pantries.

How do I get a quotation for dining chairs in Singapore?

Prices are not published. Send PANDA COLLECTIONS the product codes or specifications, quantity and delivery country and address; we can also source models that are not listed.
