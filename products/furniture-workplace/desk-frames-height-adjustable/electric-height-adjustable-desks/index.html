Electric Height-Adjustable Desks

Electric sit-stand desk frames for home and office desks, coffee tables, L-shaped and back-to-back workstations, plus complete height-adjustable desks, workstations, conference tables and a lifting podium.

Key features

  • Height ranges from about 580 to 1270 mm
  • Loads up to 2000 N on four-motor frames
  • Memory hand controllers and anti-collision
  • Black, white or grey frames

Specifications: typical values, confirmed per order; desktop sizes, frame colours and accessories to order. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.
