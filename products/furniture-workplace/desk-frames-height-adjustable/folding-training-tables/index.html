Folding Training Tables

Flip-top training tables in about 30 frame designs, with one-hand folding mechanisms, lockable castors and modesty panels; tables nest together and combine into classroom, U-shape and boardroom layouts.

Key features

  • Flip-top tables that nest for storage
  • Lockable castors
  • Folding system 2.0, 3.0 and line-controlled release
  • Modesty panels, power boxes and cable trays

Specifications: typical values, confirmed per order; desktop sizes, frame colours and accessories to order. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much space do nesting training tables save?

Folded flip-top tables nest side by side, typically taking about a quarter of the floor space of open tables when stored.
