Desk Frames & Height-Adjustable Desks

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies desk hardware and complete desks for offices, training centres, furniture manufacturers and fit-out contractors in Singapore and worldwide: single-, dual-, triple- and four-motor electric sit-stand desk frames, lifting columns and controllers, complete height-adjustable desks and workstations, flip-top folding training tables on castors, and die-cast aluminium desk frame systems for executive, conference and staff desks.

Key features

  • Electric sit-stand frames with 1 to 4 motors
  • Lifting columns in rectangular, square and round tube
  • Folding training tables that nest for storage
  • Die-cast aluminium executive and conference desk frames
  • Frames supplied for your desktops or as complete desks

Specifications: typical values, confirmed per order; desktop sizes, frame colours and accessories to order. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Single or dual motor?

Single-motor frames suit lighter home and compact desks; dual-motor frames lift faster and carry more (about 1200 N), and are recommended for office desks and larger tops.

What is the difference between 2-stage and 3-stage columns?

A 3-stage column has a lower minimum and higher maximum height (about 590–1270 mm), suiting a wider range of users; 2-stage frames typically cover about 680–1180 mm.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Request a quote with the model codes, quantities, desktop sizes and delivery location.
