Frequently Asked Questions

Which material lasts longest outdoors?

Powder-coated aluminium and cast aluminium resist rust; PE rattan and rope weaves are UV-stabilised; teak weathers to silver-grey unless oiled. Choose by climate, maintenance and style.

Do you show prices on the website?

No. Request a quote with the model codes, quantities and delivery location.
