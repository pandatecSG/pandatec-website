WHO WE ARE

Beyond Traditional Trade.

Global reach. Local support.

PANDA COLLECTIONS is the curated procurement service brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD., a company registered in Singapore. We select products and suppliers for business clients, check product quality and arrange logistics and delivery, connecting businesses with manufacturers across Asia.

We provide global sourcing, procurement, supplier coordination, OEM/ODM, quality control, consolidation, international logistics and local delivery.

From individual product requirements to multi-category project procurement, PANDA COLLECTIONS provides a practical and flexible supply solution.

What does PANDA COLLECTIONS do?
