Frequently Asked Questions – Global Sourcing & Procurement

PANDA COLLECTIONS, the curated procurement brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD., is a Singapore-based B2B global sourcing, procurement and supply-chain partner. We support businesses, corporate buyers, project owners and procurement teams with product sourcing, supplier coordination, quality control and logistics, supplying worldwide from our headquarters in Singapore.

SOURCING & PRODUCT RANGE

Sourcing and Product Range

Do you have a minimum order quantity (MOQ) for wholesale purchases?

There is no single minimum order quantity across all product categories. MOQ depends on the product type, manufacturer, production requirements and customisation options. Some standard products can be supplied in smaller quantities, while OEM/ODM products and factory-direct orders may require higher volumes. Contact PANDA COLLECTIONS with your required products and quantities to receive a suitable quotation.

SUPPLIERS, QUALITY & SAMPLES

Suppliers, Quality and Samples

How do you verify manufacturers and suppliers before placing an order?

Supplier verification is an important part of our procurement process. Depending on the product and project requirements, PANDA COLLECTIONS can coordinate checks on company information, manufacturing capabilities, product specifications, relevant certifications and production capacity. Our objective is to help customers make informed procurement decisions and reduce potential supply-chain risks before confirming an order.

Supplier verification

How do you ensure product quality before shipment?

Quality requirements are established according to the customer's approved specifications, samples, drawings and purchase requirements. Depending on the order, PANDA COLLECTIONS can coordinate production follow-ups, product inspections, packaging checks and pre-shipment quality control. Inspection scope and reporting requirements should be agreed before production or shipment. Our goal is to help ensure that supplied products meet the agreed requirements before delivery.

Quality control

Can we request product samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Sample arrangements can be discussed for products where samples are available. This is particularly useful for furniture, industrial consumables, building materials, PPE and customised products. Sample costs, shipping charges and delivery timelines depend on the product and supplier. For customised or large-volume projects, we recommend evaluating and approving samples before mass production.

QUOTATIONS & BULK ORDERS

Quotations and Bulk Orders

How long does it take to receive a quotation?

Quotation turnaround depends on product complexity, specification requirements, supplier availability and order quantity. For standard products with clear specifications, quotations can generally be prepared more quickly. Customised products, technical equipment and multi-category project enquiries may require additional supplier confirmation and technical evaluation. To help us quote accurately, please provide product descriptions, quantities, specifications, photographs or drawings, and your delivery destination.

For customers with recurring requirements, we can explore volume-based pricing, consolidated procurement and longer-term supply arrangements to improve purchasing efficiency and cost management.

LOGISTICS & SINGAPORE DELIVERY

Logistics and Singapore Delivery

Can you consolidate products from different factories into one shipment?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS can coordinate procurement and shipment consolidation for products sourced from multiple suppliers. This is particularly useful for businesses purchasing different product categories from different manufacturers. We can coordinate supplier delivery schedules, cargo preparation, consolidation arrangements and international shipping plans, subject to cargo compatibility and logistics requirements.

ConsolidationInternational logistics

Can you arrange international shipping and customs documentation?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS supports international logistics coordination, including freight arrangements, shipping documentation and delivery planning. The logistics solution depends on cargo dimensions, weight, product classification, origin, destination and required shipping terms. Import duties, taxes, permits and customs clearance responsibilities will be clarified according to the agreed shipment arrangement. We can discuss suitable air freight, sea freight and other available logistics solutions based on your requirements.

International logistics

Do you provide local delivery and installation services in Singapore?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS coordinates local delivery in Singapore for eligible products and projects. For furniture, warehouse equipment, workplace products and other project-related supplies, selected installation or assembly support can also be discussed. Please provide the delivery address, site access information, unloading requirements and installation scope when requesting a quotation. Availability and charges will be confirmed separately.

CUSTOMISATION, COMPLIANCE & PARTNERSHIP

Customisation, Compliance and Working With Us

Can you support OEM and ODM manufacturing?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS supports OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturing) and ODM (Original Design Manufacturing) enquiries, subject to product feasibility and supplier capabilities. Customers can discuss customised dimensions, materials, colours, branding, packaging, technical specifications and product designs. To assess your project, please share drawings, reference photographs, technical documents, expected quantities and any relevant quality or compliance requirements.

OEM / ODMProduct development

Can you provide product certifications, test reports and technical documentation?

Documentation can be requested and reviewed according to the specific product, manufacturer and intended application. Depending on the product, available documents may include ISO certificates, CE declarations, test reports, material specifications, safety data sheets (SDS/MSDS), technical datasheets and other relevant compliance documents. Not every document applies to every product. PANDA COLLECTIONS will confirm availability and suitability before making any compliance representations.

What payment terms do you offer for B2B customers?

Payment terms depend on the product category, supplier arrangements, order value, customer profile and transaction requirements. Depending on the agreed transaction, payment arrangements may include an advance payment with the balance payable before shipment, or other mutually agreed commercial terms. For established corporate customers and qualified business accounts, extended payment terms may be considered subject to credit assessment and approval. All payment terms will be clearly stated in the quotation or purchase agreement before order confirmation.

PANDA COLLECTIONS provides more than product sourcing.
