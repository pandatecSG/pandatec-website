Social Responsibility

PANDA COLLECTIONS, the curated procurement brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD., works with manufacturers in its supply network to give registered non-profit organisations access to essential supplies at affordable prices. The programme runs in batches, each focused on one product need, and we welcome enquiries from charities, social service agencies and community organisations.

01

Why we do it

Non-profits caring for seniors, patients and vulnerable communities buy essential consumables every month on tight budgets. We use our sourcing, quality checks and logistics to help them buy these essentials at affordable prices, so more of their funding goes to care.

02

How the batch policy works

Each batch focuses on one product need. For every batch we confirm the product, the programme pricing, the allocation available and the application period with our manufacturing partners. When a batch closes, the next batch and its product are announced on this page.

BATCH 1 · NOW OPEN

Affordable Adult Diapers for Non-Profit Organisations

For our first batch, PANDA COLLECTIONS and its manufacturing partners supply adult diapers to non-profit organisations at an affordable programme price for the people in their care.

  • Products: adult diapers, tab (taped) and pull-up types; underpads on request
  • Sizes and absorbency: confirmed with each organisation according to the people it supports
  • For: nursing homes, eldercare and day care centres, hospices, disability services and community care programmes
  • Pricing: affordable programme price for non-profit use, shared on application
  • Quality and delivery: quality checked before shipment; delivery coordinated in Singapore and the region

Apply for Batch 1   View the adult diaper product

Who can apply

  • Registered charities and Institutions of a Public Character (IPCs)
  • Social service agencies and non-profit healthcare providers
  • Nursing homes, hospices, eldercare and day care centres run on a non-profit basis
  • Community, volunteer and religious organisations providing care services

Proof of registration is requested with the application.

How to apply

  1. Email david@pandatec.sg with your organisation name and registration details.
  2. Tell us who you support and the products, sizes and estimated monthly quantity needed.
  3. We confirm eligibility, programme pricing, allocation and delivery for the current batch.
A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Social Responsibility FAQs

Who can apply to the social responsibility programme?

Registered non-profit organisations, charities and social service agencies, including eldercare homes, nursing homes, hospices, day care centres, disability services and community care groups. Proof of registration is requested with the application.

What does Batch 1 provide?

Batch 1 provides adult diapers (tab and pull-up types), with underpads available on request, at an affordable programme price for non-profit use. Sizes, absorbency levels, quantities and delivery are confirmed with each organisation.

How does the batch policy work?

Each batch focuses on one product need. For every batch PANDA COLLECTIONS confirms the product, the programme pricing, the allocation available and the application period with its manufacturing partners. When a batch closes, the next batch and its product are announced on this page.

Is the programme free of charge?

No. Products are supplied at an affordable programme price for non-profit use rather than donated. Pricing is shared with eligible organisations on application.

How do we apply?

Email david@pandatec.sg with your organisation name, registration details, the people you support, the products and sizes needed, estimated monthly quantity and delivery address.
