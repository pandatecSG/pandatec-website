A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What does PANDATEC do?

PANDATEC is a Singapore-based B2B supply chain and procurement partner connecting businesses with manufacturers and products across Asia.

Can you support customised requirements?

OEM / ODM services cover custom dimensions, materials, branding and specifications. Product-specific options need to be confirmed.

Can you coordinate multi-category orders?

Services include project procurement and consolidation of goods from multiple suppliers into one shipment.

Can you provide specifications and certificates?

Ask for documentation for the particular product and intended application. Only verified product documents will be published.

Can you arrange delivery?

PANDATEC coordinates international logistics and Singapore delivery. Timing and selected installation support are confirmed for each enquiry.
