Frequently Asked Questions

What does PANDA COLLECTIONS supply for hotels & hospitality?

3,045 catalogued products across 10 categories, including Hotel Accommodation, Sofas & Lounge Chairs, Lighting, Flooring, Outdoor Furniture, Reception & Breakout. Products outside the online catalogue can be sourced to your specification.

Do you handle wholesale, project and long-term supply?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS quotes wholesale and bulk orders, multi-category project packages and long-term framework or annual supply agreements with volume-tier pricing. Prices are quoted on request.

Do you supply outside Singapore?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS is headquartered in Singapore and supplies businesses worldwide, coordinating quality checks before shipment, consolidation and international logistics.
