A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What does PANDA COLLECTIONS supply for marine, port & shipping?

PANDA COLLECTIONS supports port operators, container and bulk terminals, shipyards, ship owners and managers, ship agents and marine contractors with port and terminal equipment, mooring and lashing gear, lifting and rigging, marine hoses, pumps and valves, spill response products, and ship chandlery: deck and engine-room stores, spares, safety equipment, chemicals, coatings and cabin stores.

Do you handle project, BOQ and recurring supply?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS quotes project packages, BOQ and material lists line by line, and recurring or framework supply. Prices are quoted on request.

Which marine buyers does PANDA COLLECTIONS support?

Port operators, terminals, shipyards, ship owners and ship management companies, ship agents, marine engineering contractors and logistics facilities.

What should a vessel supply request include?

Vessel name, IMO number if applicable, port or anchorage, ETA / ETD, maker, model and part number, quantity, photos and any certificate requirements.

Do you supply outside Singapore?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS is headquartered in Singapore and coordinates quality checks before shipment and delivery to Singapore and international destinations.
