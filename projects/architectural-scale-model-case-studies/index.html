Architectural Scale Model Case Studies: Sales Gallery, Master Plan & Equipment Models

Made-to-order architectural and display models supplied by PANDA COLLECTIONS to developers, architects, government agencies, schools and manufacturers.

By PANDA COLLECTIONS procurement team · Updated November 12, 2026 · 28 project photos

Summary

These case studies show completed models from our Architectural Scale Models range: residential sales gallery and master plan models, apartment unit layout models, regional location models, industrial and logistics park models, campus models, infrastructure and terrain models, agriculture and structure models, and equipment display models. Each model is made to order from the client's drawings, with ABS and acrylic buildings, detailed landscape, programmable LED lighting, a custom display base and a glass cover. PANDA COLLECTIONS handles quotation, drawing review, production follow-up, pre-shipment inspection and delivery to Singapore and worldwide.

Real estate sales gallery and master plan models

Large sales gallery models for residential and mixed-use developments, typically the centrepiece of a show suite. They combine realistic tower facades, detailed landscape, roads and water features, a feature display base and a glass cover, with programmed LED lighting for night-time effects.

Related products: Real Estate Sales Gallery Master Plan Model, Commercial Tower & Mixed-Use Building Model, Single Villa Architectural Model

Apartment unit layout (floor plan) models

Unit layout models show room sizes, circulation and interior finishes for each apartment type. Outer walls are usually clear acrylic so buyers can see inside, while inner walls, flooring and scaled furniture are built to the interior design scheme.

Related products: Apartment Floor Plan (Unit Layout) Model

Regional location models

Wall-mounted or table-top location models show a project in its surroundings: main roads, rail lines, rivers, parks and key amenities. Typical construction is UV-printed tempered glass or acrylic on a light-box base, with running LED lights on transport routes.

Industrial park and logistics park planning models

Planning models for industrial parks, factory campuses and logistics hubs, used in investor presentations, government briefings and corporate showrooms. They show building blocks, internal roads, loading areas, green space and phasing.

Related products: Industrial Park Planning Model

Infrastructure and terrain models

Expressway, bridge and waterway models for infrastructure projects, plus terrain and national-layout models for corporate and planning exhibitions. Terrain is built up in layers to show slopes, water and vegetation.

Related products: Terrain & Infrastructure Model

School and university campus models

Campus master plan models for universities, polytechnics and schools, showing teaching blocks, dormitories, sports fields and future phases.

Related products: Campus & Master Plan Model

Smart agriculture, structures and sports venue models

Models for agricultural parks, greenhouses, tent and membrane structures and domed sports halls, built for exhibitions, tenders and visitor centres.

Related products: Smart Agriculture & Farm Planning Model, Stadium & Landmark Building Model

Equipment and plant room models

Cut-away models of plant rooms, process lines and machinery for trade shows and sales teams, showing equipment layout, piping and flow in a format that is easy to transport.

Related products: Equipment & Machinery Display Model

How to order an architectural model

  1. Send your brief: drawings, preferred scale or table size, lighting, base style, delivery location and deadline, through Request a Quote.
  2. Quotation and proposal: we confirm scale, level of detail, materials and lead time and send a quotation.
  3. Drawing review and production: production drawings are prepared for your approval, and we share progress photos for your comments.
  4. Inspection and shipment: the finished model is checked before packing in custom crates for export.
  5. Delivery and installation: delivery to Singapore or your project country, with installation arranged as agreed.

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed to quote an architectural scale model?

Send the drawings (site plan, elevations and landscape plan, in CAD or PDF), the model scale or the size of the display area, the lighting and base you want, the delivery location and the date the model must be installed.

Which scales are used for architectural models?

Sales gallery master plan models are commonly 1:100 to 1:300, single buildings and villas 1:50 to 1:100, apartment unit layout models 1:20 to 1:30, and industrial park or regional models 1:500 or smaller. The scale is set by the site size and the space available in the gallery.

What materials are architectural scale models made of?

Buildings are made from 0.5–5.0 mm ABS sheet and acrylic, cut by CNC engraving and laser machines and spray-finished to match facade colours. Landscape uses scale trees, figures, vehicles and simulated water, and models are finished with a display base and a glass or acrylic cover.

Can the model include lighting effects?

Yes. Buildings can be fitted with LED lighting that switches between preset levels (for example 30%, 50%, 70% and 100% of windows lit), with warm lighting for retail podiums, blue lighting for water features and running lights on roads, controlled by switches or a control panel.

Do you supply architectural models to Singapore and overseas?

Yes. PANDA COLLECTIONS coordinates drawings, production follow-up, quality checks before shipment, export packing and delivery to Singapore and other countries. Installation and after-sales support are confirmed with each quotation.
