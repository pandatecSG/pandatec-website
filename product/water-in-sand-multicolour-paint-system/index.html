Product description

Water-in-Sand Multicolour Paint System: a water-in-sand multicolour (sand-texture granite-effect) paint system, available in 62 standard colours. Coloured granules and fine sand in a water-based binder build a textured granite or sandstone finish with more body than water-in-water paint, for façades, podiums and boundary walls.

System build-up:

Layer 1 – alkali-resistant sealer primer (clear): 5566C-1301, 20 kg pail, approx. 0.12 kg/m², 1 coat, touch-dry 2 h

Layer 2 – black base coat: 5566X-1301, 20 kg pail, approx. 0.12 kg/m², 1 coat, touch-dry 2 h

Layer 3 – tinted mid coat: 5566D-20Z, 25 kg pail, approx. 0.30 kg/m², 1 coat, dry 2 h

Layer 4 – multicolour sand coat: 5566D-20S, 25 kg pail, approx. 1.0–1.2 kg/m², 2 coats with a 3.5–4.0 mm spray gun, dry 3–24 h

Layer 5 – clear topcoat: 5566C-1501B, 20 kg pail, approx. 0.10 kg/m², 1 coat, dry 2 h

Standard colours (62): 5566D-2001, 5566D-2002, 5566D-2003, 5566D-2004, 5566D-2004-3, 5566D-2005, 5566D-2006, 5566D-2007, 5566D-2008, 5566D-2009, 5566D-2011, 5566D-2012, 5566D-2013, 5566D-2014, 5566D-2016, 5566D-2017, 5566D-2018, 5566D-2019, 5566D-2021, 5566D-2022, 5566D-2023, 5566D-2024, 5566D-2025, 5566D-2026, 5566D-2027, 5566D-2028, 5566D-2029, 5566D-2030, 5566D-2031, 5566D-2031-2, 5566D-2032, 5566D-2033, 5566D-2034, 5566D-2035, 5566D-2036, 5566D-2037, 5566D-2039, 5566D-2039-1, 5566D-2040, 5566D-2041, 5566D-2042, 5566D-2043, 5566D-2044, 5566D-2046, 5566D-2049, 5566D-2050, 5566D-2051, 5566D-2052, 5566D-2053, 5566D-2054, 5566D-2056, 5566D-2057, 5566D-2058, 5566D-2058-1, 5566D-2060, 5566D-2061, 5566D-2062, 5566D-2064, 5566D-2065, 5566D-2066, 5566D-2069, 5566D-2070
