Hospital Bed SK-CHD01 Super Low Children BedSKD-C

Product code: PT-BED-SK-CHD01  ·  Medical & Healthcare

The Super Low Children Hospital Bed SKD-C is a premium electrically operated pediatric hospital bed designed to maximize patient safety, comfort, and caregiver convenience. Featuring an ultra-low sleeping position, electric profiling functions, wooden safety side rails, and a robust steel frame, it is ideal for pediatric wards, children’s hospitals, rehabilitation centers, and long-term care facilities where fall prevention and patient comfort are priorities.

Dimensions:

External Size: 2080(L) × 930(W) × 190–610(H) mm

Mattress Platform: 1900 × 780 mm

Safe Working Load: 215 kg

Adjustments:

Height Adjustment: 190–610 mm

Backrest Adjustment: 0–74°

Knee-rest Adjustment: 0–34°

Reverse Trendelenburg: 0–12°

Auto-contour Function

Technical Configuration:

Five linear motors

One remote controller

Powder-coated steel mattress platform

One set of wooden three-section side rails

One set of wooden head and foot boards

Four 3-inch castors

Features:

Ultra-low minimum height of 190 mm significantly reduces the risk of injury from accidental falls

Five high-performance linear motors provide smooth, quiet, and precise electric adjustment of all bed functions

Auto-contour function automatically adjusts the backrest and knee-rest simultaneously to improve comfort and reduce patient sliding

Premium wooden three-section side rails feature a two-step unlocking mechanism for enhanced child safety

Aluminum alloy side rail tracks ensure smooth, reliable operation and long-term durability

High-quality wooden head and foot boards are available in various finishes to create a warm, child-friendly healthcare environment

Powder-coated steel mattress platform provides excellent structural strength and corrosion resistance

Four 3-inch castors allow smooth bed movement and easy transport within healthcare facilities

Foldable design simplifies transportation, storage, and handling when required

Remote controller enables caregivers to conveniently operate all electric adjustment functions

Durable, easy-to-clean surfaces support effective infection control and routine sanitization

Suitable for pediatric wards, children’s hospitals, rehabilitation centers, long-term care facilities, and specialist healthcare environments

Designed with patient safety, comfort, and advanced functionality in mind, the Super Low Children Hospital Bed SKD-C provides a dependable pediatric care solution that minimizes fall risks while enhancing clinical efficiency and caregiver convenience.
