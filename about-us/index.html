WHO WE ARE

Beyond Traditional Trade

Helping enterprises source smarter, spend less and receive on time.

PANDATEC is a professional supply chain management and procurement services company headquartered in Singapore. We provide businesses worldwide with one-stop procurement, supply chain integration and international trade solutions.

With extensive industry experience, a mature supply chain system and a network of qualified suppliers across China, Southeast Asia and other markets, we have provided long-term, reliable procurement services to large enterprises, listed companies and multinational clients, helping them reduce procurement costs, improve supply efficiency and build secure, transparent and sustainable supply chains.

PANDA COLLECTIONS is the curated procurement service brand of PANDATEC PTE. LTD. (UEN 201541665H), established in Singapore in 2015.

WHAT DRIVES US

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Our Mission

To make procurement simpler, more cost-effective and more reliable for businesses, by connecting them with the right suppliers and managing every step from sourcing to delivery.

Our Vision

To become a leading Singapore-headquartered supply chain partner for enterprises worldwide, building more efficient, reliable and sustainable global supply chains.

Our Core Values

  • Customer first – Every sourcing decision starts from the client's requirement.
  • Integrity & transparency – Clear pricing, honest communication and traceable documents.
  • Quality without compromise – Controlled from supplier selection to final inspection.
  • Efficiency – Fast responses, on-time delivery, no wasted steps.
  • Long-term partnership – Mutual benefit and lasting relationships over one-off deals.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

What does PANDATEC do?

PANDATEC is a supply chain management and procurement services company headquartered in Singapore. Through its brand PANDA COLLECTIONS, it provides businesses worldwide with one-stop procurement, supply chain integration and international trade solutions, from supplier sourcing and quality control to OEM / ODM, import and export, warehousing, logistics and delivery.

Where is PANDATEC based and where do you source from?

PANDATEC PTE. LTD. is headquartered at 35 Tannery Road, Singapore. Our supplier network covers China, Southeast Asia and other international markets.

Do you offer payment terms?

Yes. Depending on the scale of cooperation and the client's qualification, we can offer flexible payment terms, including 30, 45 or 60-day credit terms.

Can you support customised requirements?

Yes. OEM / ODM services cover custom dimensions, materials, branding and specifications. Product-specific options are confirmed for each enquiry.

Can you coordinate multi-category orders?

Yes. We bring multiple product categories under one supply relationship and consolidate goods from multiple suppliers into one shipment.

Can you provide specifications and certificates?

Ask for documentation for the particular product and intended application. Only verified product documents are issued and published.

Can you arrange delivery?

Yes. We manage international freight, import and export documentation, warehousing and delivery. Timing and installation support are confirmed for each enquiry.
