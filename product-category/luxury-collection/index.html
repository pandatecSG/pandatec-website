Sofas & Lounge Chairs

PANDA COLLECTIONS supplies commercial sofas, lounge chairs and armchairs for hotel lobbies, executive offices, showrooms, clubhouses and residential projects in Singapore and worldwide. The range covers top-grain leather and fabric sofas, channel-tufted and Chesterfield styles, swivel and tub armchairs, Modern Oriental solid-wood sets, and matching coffee tables, side tables and accent pieces, with sizes, upholstery and finishes confirmed per project.

Key features

  • Sofas: 1-, 2- and 3-seater, corner and chaise configurations in top-grain leather or fabric
  • Lounge chairs: armchairs, swivel chairs, tub and barrel chairs, sling and safari chairs
  • Coffee & side tables: marble, solid wood, forged iron and leather-top designs
  • Modern Oriental solid-wood sofa sets with matching chairs, tables and TV cabinets
  • Custom upholstery, colour and size options for hospitality and office fit-outs

Materials & documents: upholstery material, frame, foam density and finish stated per product; fabric and leather samples available on request. Pricing: on request – customised quotation.
